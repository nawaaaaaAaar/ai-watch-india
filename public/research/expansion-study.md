# AI Watch India: Synthetic Media and AI Governance Expansion

This expansion adds two official-document collections to the original DPDP desk: synthetic-media/platform amendments and the India AI Governance Guidelines. It contains 26 new comparison units and 18 additional research briefs. Evidence and status were checked on 5 October 2026; this is a bounded desk study, not an independent legal opinion or verification of institutional implementation.

## What the two collections contribute

- **Synthetic-media rules:** A draft-to-final change desk with clearly labelled prior-law comparisons for changes that were not proposed in the October draft. The official notification specifies commencement on 20 February 2026, and subsequent English corrections must remain part of its evidence chain ([notified amendment](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/f55fe52418b03f58b0669f6a8bc03b6d.pdf), [corrigenda](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/20c30107195f68865104dd4e16176f4d.pdf)).
- **AI governance guidelines:** A recommendation-lineage desk, not a certified legal redline. The January consultation report contains six recommendations; the November framework reorganises governance into principles, six policy pillars, an action plan and practical guidance ([consultation report](https://indiaai.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/docs/subcommittee-report-dec26.pdf), [published guidelines](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc2025115685601.pdf)).
- **Decision preparation:** Each comparison preserves a reporting question and limitations alongside wording. Institutional appointment orders, actual reporting routes, detector accuracy and implementation outcomes remain matters to investigate, not conclusions inferred from policy prose.

## Synthetic-media collection

### Sources and version discipline

The consultation document is the October 2025 draft with five proposed amending rules, not the similarly named final notification ([official draft](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/9de47fb06522b9e40a61e4731bc7de51.pdf)). The final G.S.R. 120(E) also has five amending rules, but changes many more provisions than the draft proposed ([official notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/f55fe52418b03f58b0669f6a8bc03b6d.pdf)).

For additions absent from the draft, the desk uses the previous consolidated text and labels the comparison “Prior law to amended law”; it does not invent missing draft language ([prior consolidated IT Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/708f6a344c74249c2e1bbb6890342f80.pdf)). That consolidated snapshot includes an October amendment with its own operative-date annotation, which must not be confused with the SGI notification's commencement.

The February corrigendum has two directed English substitutions on Gazette page 10 and separate Hindi-language substitutions. This English-text register records the two English substitutions, not a combined count that implies the same changes in both languages ([correcting instrument](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/20c30107195f68865104dd4e16176f4d.pdf)). The corrected consolidation and FAQ are supporting sources, not replacements for original Gazette wording ([corrected consolidation](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/0b576f2071694b52e4cd6bb1b6dfab1e.pdf), [MeitY FAQ](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/065b6deb585441b5ccdf8be42502a49c.pdf)).

### What deserves original reporting

- **Appeals and misclassification:** Ask platforms for false-positive rates, review procedures and notices to affected users. The duty to deploy measures is not proof that detection is accurate or fair.
- **Clock routing:** Test whether real complaints enter the correct grievance category rather than applying one headline deadline to every request. The notification uses distinct actual-knowledge and grievance routes ([amendment](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/f55fe52418b03f58b0669f6a8bc03b6d.pdf)).
- **Label survivability:** Check prominence, accessibility and provenance after cropping, reposting or format conversion. These are questions for implementation evidence, not answers supplied by a policy document.
- **Civil-society concerns:** WITNESS welcomes some narrowing and labelling changes while criticising foundational enforcement and transparency gaps; its analysis is an advocacy perspective, not enacted wording ([WITNESS analysis](https://www.witness.org/indias-synthetic-media-rules-build-enforcement-on-the-wrong-foundation/)).

An August 2026 government response continues to describe the SGI amendments and November AI guidelines as parts of the regulatory approach. This supports recency context, but does not certify the absence of later amendments or judicial orders ([PIB response](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295500&reg=1&lang=1)).

### Implementation questions worth pursuing

- **Institutions:** Locate appointment orders, terms of reference, membership, budgets and published outputs. The final report calls AISI recently established while recommending AIGG/TPEC; those statements are preserved without inventing current operational status ([framework, Part 2.6](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc2025115685601.pdf)).
- **Incident reporting:** Verify an actual reporting route, confidentiality design and feedback loop. Do not assume that an incident repository resolves individual grievances.
- **Accountability:** Ask for public transparency reports, audits and redress outcomes, distinguishing self-certification from independently checked practice.
- **Policy chronology:** The November framework discusses draft DPDP rules and unresolved copyright questions. Later instruments must be checked separately, not silently inserted into the historical report ([framework, Part 2.3](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc2025115685601.pdf)).

## Application and verification boundaries

The expanded desk preserves the original 30 DPDP records and adds 14 SGI units and 12 AI-guideline units. Ten SGI briefs and eight AI-governance briefs join the original twelve, making 30 reusable briefs rather than artificially counting every continuity or citation-cleanup record as a contribution.

Comparison screens, searches, family filters, source links, notebooks and exports now retain collection identity and comparison kind. AI lineage has automatic word highlighting disabled; tables and the action plan are not scored as legal deletions. Corrections are directed to mapped English segments, and original print remains available.

All thirteen source records include extracted-text snapshots and SHA-256 hashes. These are text snapshots, not original PDF binary archives; a matching hash proves text-file integrity, not legal authenticity or extraction perfection. Machine-readable data and reproduction scripts accompany the study.

No interviews, newsroom usability tests, institutional adoption verification, paid service or public-domain launch was performed. Court status, all later amendments, implementation outcomes and bilingual equivalence are not certified. These limits are kept visible rather than hidden behind a total coverage count.
