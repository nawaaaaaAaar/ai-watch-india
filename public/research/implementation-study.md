# AI Watch India: Governance Implementation Evidence

This investigation adds ten implementation checkpoints to the policy-change desk. The question is not simply whether a proposal appears in a later press release, but what documentary milestone it establishes and what outcome still needs verification. Records were reviewed on 5 October 2026; no agency confirmations were requested.

## Principal finding

The evidence does not support a single implemented/not-implemented verdict. AIGEG and TPEC have constitution memoranda dated 13 April 2026, while their public announcements are dated 16 and 18 April respectively ([AIGEG order](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/04/43a4ec455c26b0f061cc7cca98770a45.pdf), [AIGEG announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2252739&reg=1&lang=1), [TPEC order](https://d12aarmt01l54a.cloudfront.net/cms/files/constitution-of-tpec/1776447206.pdf), [TPEC announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2253322&reg=1&lang=1)).

The Safety Institute has documented administrative processes and official creation claims, but the reviewed statements do not settle its operational readiness. The 27 July release says it “has been established,” while the 29 July release says it “is being established”; those formulations may describe different milestones rather than prove that one statement is false ([27 July explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2289946&reg=3&lang=1), [29 July explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2291062&reg=48&lang=1)).

This distinction is the desk's practical contribution: identify the strongest documented action, preserve the qualification, and provide a concrete next evidence request. These are ten checkpoints, not ten new institutions, ten discovered failures or ten claims of exclusive novelty.

## Findings useful for reporting and decisions

### Formal institutions versus institutional delivery

The AIGEG order's terms include cross-government coordination, regulatory-gap analysis, a deployment roadmap with labour considerations and classification of use cases as deploy, pilot or defer. Its composition includes ten seats, with the Electronics & IT minister as chair and the ministry secretary as member convener ([AIGEG memorandum](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/04/43a4ec455c26b0f061cc7cca98770a45.pdf)). Those are identifiable responsibilities to trace through agendas, decisions, guidelines and expenditure records; the order does not itself demonstrate delivery.

The TPEC order provides a six-seat advisory composition and technical/policy functions, while the announcement describes its support to AIGEG. It should not be reported as a general statutory AI licensing authority merely because it advises governance decisions ([TPEC memorandum](https://d12aarmt01l54a.cloudfront.net/cms/files/constitution-of-tpec/1776447206.pdf), [TPEC announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2253322&reg=1&lang=1)). A useful enquiry would request delivered risk assessments, recommendations, disclosures and action-taken records, not just another description of the committee's purpose.

### Recruitment and partnerships versus capacity

The director notice lists model evaluation, red-teaming, risk assessment and operationalising the hub-and-spoke model among the role's responsibilities ([DIC recruitment notice](https://dic.gov.in/jobs/director-indiaai-safety-institute/)). The notice demonstrates recruitment, not the appointee's existence, tenure or resources; its deadline is historical and must not be presented as an open opportunity.

The partner EOI lists publication on 9 May 2025 and submission by 9 June 2025, requires partner institutions to support 50% of estimated total project cost, and anticipates agreements defining work allocation. It also anticipates publication of collective outputs with a confidentiality exception, including national-security concerns ([EOI sections 6.2, 6.5, 7 and 8](https://indiaai.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/docs/sg-call-for-partnerships-indiaai-safety-institute.pdf)). Selection lists, contracts, disbursements and released outputs are the appropriate next milestones; an absent public report does not automatically establish inactivity.

### Selection versus technical effectiveness

The five second-round selections are Saakshya; AI Vishleshak; a real-time voice deepfake detection system; an agriculture-LLM gender-bias project; and Anvil, a penetration-testing/evaluation project ([official selection table](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2175698)). The table identifies applicant organisations but does not provide reproducible benchmark results; a reporter should request data, baselines, error distributions, licence, release and acceptance evidence before claiming a tool solves a problem.

The October 2025 selection predates the November governance framework, and the July 2026 explanation reports a wider total of 13 approved projects. These are related programme records, not five additional projects beyond the thirteen or proof that the November framework caused the earlier selection ([selection record](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2175698), [July explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2289946&reg=3&lang=1)). Likewise, the mission's overall outlay cannot be treated as a Safety Institute budget or project-level spending without a corresponding allocation record.

### Three different reporting and remedy questions

The governance guidelines propose a national incident database capable of collecting from local databases using a common schema, and they keep grievance redress separate from that learning mechanism ([guidelines Parts 2.4 and 2.5](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc2025115685601.pdf)). The review did not verify a live national AI-harm intake mechanism; this is a bounded evidence gap, not a declaration that none exists.

CERT-In's 2022 directions require the specified entity classes to report listed cyber incidents within six hours of noticing or being informed of them, and Annexure I includes attacks or suspicious activity affecting AI/ML-related systems. This is not a universal six-hour deadline for every unfair or harmful AI decision, nor evidence that the broader proposed database has launched ([directions clause (ii) and Annexure I](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf)).

The July deepfake explanation points to intermediary grievance officers and GAC appeals for relevant content disputes. Those routes are not interchangeable with a general appeal against an employer's, lender's or hospital's automated decision ([grievance explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2291671&reg=3&lang=1)). Before routing a complaint, identify the decision, responsible organisation, governing sector and applicable procedure; this desk is not a legal case-routing service.

### Commitment versus compliance

The March summit report states that 13 leading global and Indian frontier-model developers announced New Delhi Frontier AI Impact Commitments, and July reporting repeats the existence of voluntary commitments. The records also describe other summit initiatives, which should not be conflated with every domestic voluntary framework recommended by the guidelines ([March summit report](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2234343&reg=3&lang=1), [July explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2289946&reg=3&lang=1)). The next question is what each participant promised and demonstrably delivered, not how many pledges can be counted.

## Website implementation

The release includes an implementation-ledger view with status and search filters; ten full evidence pages with multiple documentary items; links back to the underlying recommendations; and a records-to-verify checklist for each checkpoint. Individual, collection and mixed-policy Markdown/CSV briefs preserve status, evidence, interpretation and caveats; session notebooks retain local review notes.

Automatic word-diff and corrected-version toggles are disabled for this collection. A proposal-to-record evidence trail is not a legal redline, and no official correction is invented where none was registered. No enquiries, information requests, grievance submissions or correction proposals are sent automatically.

## Method and limits

The corpus contains twelve additional official source snapshots plus the previously included governance framework, with a parliamentary response and September journalism read as cross-checks. The parliamentary response independently reproduces the official establishment claim; September reporting about possible tighter incident rules describes prospective action, not proof that new rules were enacted ([Lok Sabha response dated 22 July](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS50_JZTaVS.pdf), [MediaNama's 22 September report](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-india-tighter-ai-incident-reporting-rules/)).

Dates come from documents and their posted-date fields, not search-result timestamps. Source extraction sometimes includes duplicates, flattened tables or OCR errors; snapshots preserve those limitations, and the constitution PDFs should be inspected when quoting composition or exact legal text.

The linked TPEC PDF failed SDK extraction because of a robots restriction, then succeeded through direct public download and local text extraction. A named October opinion article failed retrieval and was not used as factual evidence; an AIKosh contact-page extraction omitted dynamic contact values, which were likewise not used to assert names or a universal complaint route.

The companion search log records questions, sources and unresolved boundaries. This is not an exhaustive public-record or court-status search, independent legal review, operational audit, proof of tool effectiveness or user-demand study; it completes a ten-checkpoint curated implementation investigation.
