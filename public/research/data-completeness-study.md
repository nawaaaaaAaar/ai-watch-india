# AI Watch India: Complete Curated Data Audit

Edition 05. Checked 5 October 2026, India date. This audit makes the entire stored corpus accessible, identifies its boundaries and adds evidence where the follow-up research could support it. “Complete” describes the included curated corpus, not every Indian AI deployment or every record an institution holds.

## What is now included

- **All 72 records:** thirty DPDP provision comparisons, fourteen synthetic-media comparisons, twelve governance recommendation comparisons, ten implementation checkpoints and six public-service case files. Every record has a full downloadable dossier including all stored fields; the 46 selected contribution briefs remain a separate editorial subset.
- **All 46 source records:** forty earlier source records, five additional public-service sources and an extracted DPDP parent Act. Dates, source types, original URLs, snapshot scope and snapshot hashes are exported, not hidden in interface code.
- **All ten directed English corrections:** corrected and printed wording remains distinct. This is not a bilingual legal audit or proof that no further correction exists.
- **All 72 public-service accountability slots:** six cases retain the same twelve dimensions, including partial, conflicting and unverified answers.
- **All 185 evidence items:** a flattened register of stored before/after excerpts and implementation/case evidence trails. Repeated excerpts can support more than one record; this is not a count of unique documents or independent findings.
- **All 140 limitation entries:** every record's caution, every case dimension that is not fully documented, and every implementation record queue. These are overlapping limitations, not 140 proven harms or unique absent documents.
- **All research packages and original DPDP raw registers:** the raw machine comparison remains labelled raw rather than upgraded to a verified legal finding.
- **Integrity manifest and complete ZIP:** included file paths, sizes and SHA-256 hashes are reproducible. The ZIP includes its manifest and the manifest checksum; the archive's separate checksum covers the ZIP bytes.

These counts are repository inventory values checked by the automated tests. The archive does not include private notebook notes, application dependencies, original PDF binaries, full copyrighted articles or records that were not retrieved.

## What the follow-up research changed

### National clinical support now has a study lead, not a transplanted local accuracy score

A national implementation-study abstract describes development of a knowledge-based clinical decision-support framework during 2022–2024, expert-clinician validation and implementation in eSanjeevani 2.0. This is relevant evidence about the national system, unlike the previously retained local study recommending integration. [National study abstract and declarations](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.22.25340800)

The retrieved abstract does not supply a complete independent error-rate or subgroup-performance dossier. Full manuscript access failed through both the content-fetch route and a browser check; the record is labelled “Research abstract; full manuscript not reviewed,” without claiming peer-review status. [National study record](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.22.25340800)

The declaration “This study did not receive any funding” concerns the study. It does not establish zero development or deployment cost, and the funding dimension remains partial. [Study declaration](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.22.25340800)

### Developer and official implementation dates remain visibly different

Wadhwani's healthcare page describes integration since March 2023. The parliamentary response records April 2023; the case retains both descriptions without silently resolving them or assuming they describe the same milestone. [Developer account](https://www.wadhwaniai.org/impact/healthcare/) and [parliamentary answer](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS284_ZZWF4R.pdf?source=lsapps)

The developer's MIT Solve submission describes rule-based SMART-form work and collaboration with C-DAC. This supports a supplier/developer lead, not an executed production procurement award or proof of the current contractual arrangement. [Developer submission](https://solve.mit.edu/solutions/73043)

### Historical clinical and judicial safeguards now have their own evidence records

The 25 March 2020 telemedicine guidance states that AI/ML platforms cannot independently counsel or prescribe and provides for a grievance mechanism. These are documented historical requirements, not verification of today's override logs or a functioning clinical-error appeal pathway. [Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, sections 5.4 and 5.6](https://esanjeevani.mohfw.gov.in/assets/guidelines/Telemedicine_Practice_Guidelines.pdf)

The court-circulated translation guidelines provide correctness checks by translators/reviewers and identify High Court grants or state-government funding for remuneration. They broaden the evidence beyond the earlier Allahabad notice, but do not establish uniform current implementation, expenditure or a national SUVAS software contract. [Circulated translation guidelines, guidelines 4 and 10](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec03ac73001b1d44f4925449ce09d9f5/uploads/2023/09/2023092013.pdf)

### Statutory context is no longer only an external link

The parent Digital Personal Data Protection Act now has a separately catalogued extracted-text snapshot with its original government URL and a hash of the included UTF-8 text. This does not turn the Act into an additional comparison unit, certify OCR or replace the official instrument. [Official DPDP Act](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)

## Search scope and access failures

The follow-up ran eleven focused queries covering UPSC mismatch procedures and retention, CyberGuard production evaluation and procurement, SUPACE final guidance, SUVAS corrections, eSanjeevani evaluation and grievances, and MahaCrimeOS contracts and rollout. The discovery register retains the returned query, title and URL; it is a search log, not a catalogue of verified evidence.

Selected sources were fetched and read before the new excerpts entered the corpus. A DOI lookup and a title-specific search supplied the study lead; an additional policing article was read as a cross-check but was not promoted into another corpus source. Some broad procurement search results were irrelevant and were not used as evidence of absence.

The current eSanjeevani support page and the full study manuscript could not be inspected successfully. The retrieval log records the failures, the partial DOI abstract retrieval and the successful source reads; no complaint form, inquiry or information request was submitted.

## How to use the data room

Download the complete ZIP and the archive checksum from the data room. Verify the ZIP bytes first, then inspect `data-room/manifest.json` for every included payload file; the separate manifest checksum avoids a recursive self-hash.

Use `combined-data.json` for the application's full structured dataset, `records.jsonl` for exact record objects and the dossier files for readable context plus all stored fields. CSV registers are for filtering and review; the JSON dataset remains the authority where CSV cells flatten arrays.

Filter record coverage by collection, inspect all records rather than only selected briefs, and search the limitation register for dimensions such as procurement, retention or appeal. A zero-result filter means no matching row, not that all evidence is complete.

Open the original source before quoting a larger passage or making a legal, clinical or operational claim. Hashes establish file integrity, not source truth, complete extraction, current law, independent validation or effective deployment.

## Remaining limits and maintenance

This is a curated five-collection desk, not a census. Current institution-specific SOPs, executed awards, full clinical validation material, budget records, operational logs and remedy evidence remain unresolved where the case fields say so.

Source URLs may change and publication text can evolve. Each included snapshot preserves the checked excerpts or extracted text with its scope stated; source retrieval is not a substitute for interviews, official responses, registry confirmation or independent audit.

The completeness workflow adds evidence without creating fake findings. Unknown fields remain in the dataset and exports until a reviewed source supports an update, and future updates must preserve prior-version uncertainty rather than overwrite it silently.
