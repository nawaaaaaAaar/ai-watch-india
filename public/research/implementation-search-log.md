# AI Watch India: Implementation Search Scope

Review date: 5 October 2026. The following scope explains the limits of “not established by reviewed records”; it must not be used as proof that an institution, appointment or mechanism does not exist.

Broad searches covered AI governance implementation in 2026; AIGEG constitution; Safety Institute projects and partners; a national AI incident database; voluntary commitments; TPEC recommendations; Safe & Trusted AI projects; and September/October recency.

Follow-up queries included:

## Cross-checks and retrieval limits

[September incident-reporting journalism](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-india-tighter-ai-incident-reporting-rules/) was read to avoid missing recent developments, but prospective measures were not converted into enacted duties. A search result for an October Safety Institute opinion article could not be fetched and was excluded from the evidence basis.

The [AIKosh contact page](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/support/contact-us) exposed a grievance form in extracted text but omitted its dynamic contact values. It was not treated as a universal national AI complaint route or used to name an official.

The TPEC constitution link was extracted from PIB. SDK retrieval returned a robots error; a fallback public download followed by local `pdftotext -layout` succeeded. OCR errors remain visible in the snapshot rather than being silently corrected.

## What this review does not establish

- AIGEG or TPEC meeting activity, current membership substitutions, completed recommendations or action taken.
- A completed director appointment or the Safety Institute's current staffing, spending, operational readiness or testing capacity.
- A partner award list, executed agreements or project-by-project disbursements.
- Tool licences, release availability, reproducible benchmark performance or successful deployment.
- A live national AI-harm repository intake endpoint, universal AI-specific redress or completed voluntary-commitment obligations.

These are the next verification targets, not findings of absence. No RTI, information request, interview, complaint or other enquiry was submitted.
