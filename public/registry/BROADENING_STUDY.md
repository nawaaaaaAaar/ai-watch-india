# AI Watch: doubling the system registry without doubling the claims

Release 1.2.0, documentary cutoff 5 October 2026. This round adds thirty-six named system/service families to the previous thirty-six-record release. The practical contribution is a larger joinable evidence dataset with preserved research denominators, not a claim that seventy-two AI deployments are currently operational or independently validated.

## What the new evidence changes

- **Operational continuity**: September reporting says Safe Kerala's challan processing was suspended while cameras continued recording. An older launch cannot be treated as continuous current enforcement, and suspension of processing is not the same as every camera being off. ([September reporting](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/kerala/2026/Sep/20/keralas-ai-camera-project-stalls-as-govt-dues-mount-traffic-management-takes-a-hit))
- **Public money and stage**: Delhi ITMS's Rs 1,789.52 crore is a cabinet-approved estimated project cost, with future competitive supplier selection and implementation. It is not expenditure, a reviewed award or achieved deployment; the Delhi traffic system is not the railway track-inspection ITMS. ([Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2316953))
- **Risk flags and effects**: FRI assigns mobile-number risk categories and influences financial-institution decisions. Its reported suspected losses prevented do not establish false-positive rates, actual counterfactual losses avoided or remedies for wrongly blocked customers. Its official source describes analytical risk classification, so model-specific AI remains qualified rather than assumed. ([DoT explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2307871))
- **Clinical evaluation**: Earlier iOncology reporting gives a clinician-comparison accuracy claim; September reporting explicitly retains research-mode/pilot validation limits. An internal benchmark and prospective clinical utility are different questions, not numbers to reconcile into certified readiness. ([Earlier clinician account](https://indianexpress.com/article/long-reads/aiims-ai-project-cancer-therapy-9169481/), [September validation limits](https://health.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/health-it/indian-ai-platform-for-cancer-care-featured-in-brics-health-journal/134250031))
- **Outcome attribution**: iRASTE's implementing-partner report describes a 200-bus observational comparison and lower fatal-accident crash rate. Saagu Baagu's programme partner reports crop gains across a bundled intervention; neither becomes a verified randomised effect attributable solely to AI. ([iRASTE report](https://inai.ai/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Executive-Summary-iRASTE-Telangana.pdf), [Saagu Baagu account](https://www.weforum.org/impact/ai-for-agriculture-in-india/))
- **Tender safeguards**: The Odisha helpline RFP requires quality checks and approval before disseminating AI-generated advice. That is inspectable accountability evidence, not proof of actual compliance or an awarded contract; its separate “AI” artificial-insemination references are not machine-learning evidence. ([Helpline RFP](https://home.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-03/Krushi%20Samrudhi%20Helpline%20(RFP)-%20FINAL_0.pdf))
- **Regulatory functions**: SEBI's annual report separates public-content surveillance, advertisement compliance and investigation summarisation. SUDARSAN, R(AI)DAR and InfoMerge are distinct workflows; detected posts are not independently adjudicated fraud, and generic cybersecurity or e-adjudication software is not labelled AI merely because it appears nearby. ([SEBI technology chapter](https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/publications/aug-2026/Chapter%2009.pdf))
- **Human routes and alternatives**: Jeevan Pramaan's FAQ retains existing life-certificate routes and directs rejected certificates to the Pension Disbursing Agency. This is more useful than merely saying the app exists, but it does not establish successful biometric-error remedy outcomes. ([Official FAQ](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in/v2.0/misc/faq))

## Method and quality boundaries

Discovery and identity follow-ups were followed by candidate-specific recency and accountability searches. The round logs 153 queries, 693 returned discovery hits and 100 source retrieval receipts, including three failed pages. Forty-nine used new sources were checked before coding; selected excerpts are limited to 250 unique words per new source. No unretrieved page is treated as read and no search snippet becomes a coded fact.

The thirty-six additions are a purposive broadening cohort. The earlier five-ministry-and-independent-court frame remains a separate 26-record association, not a denominator for the new sectors/states. Eighteen original seed records, eighteen institutional additions and thirty-six broadening additions are identified by `research_round`; neither institutional associations nor partner roles prove legal ownership.

Each new system has all twelve fields, including explicit `n.a.` where an executed supplier award, current benchmark, retention policy, override procedure or appeal outcome was not established. The result is not a list of seventy-two verified operational AI systems. Named proposed, historical, suspended, experimental and evaluation-infrastructure records remain visible as such.

Family deduplication is explicit: legacy Trinetra and YAKSH are one new UP-police family record, and iRASTE's regional observations are one programme-family record rather than extra installations. Internal app/voice/chat channels are not extra MahaVISTAAR systems. Named domain services linked to foundational platforms retain their distinct institutional function and relationship.

Current coding remains single-analyst research. The original fifty-source recheck and 216 assertion triages retain their original scope; no fresh independent recheck of all current sources or inter-rater review is invented. Causal, national-prevalence and legal-compliance conclusions are not supported by this selected corpus.

## How to reuse this release

Use `systems.research_round` for cohort comparisons, `evidence_links` for provenance and typed metric/evaluation fields before comparing numerical claims. Do not pool proposed projects, normative thresholds, administrative reach, observational outcomes and diagnostic benchmarks into one performance measure. Earlier policy comparisons remain supporting collections with different units.

The codebook explains each table and the SQL includes examples. The package preserves complete dossiers, selected source snapshots, all three research rounds' logs and checksum manifests; it does not mirror full papers/PDFs or citizen data. No public launch, paid service, institutional enquiry or independent second coding was undertaken.
