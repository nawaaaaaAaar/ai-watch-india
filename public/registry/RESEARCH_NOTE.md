# AI Watch: what deployment evidence actually establishes

India institution-and-system dataset, version 1.0.0. Documentary research checked 5 October 2026; eighteen purposively selected named systems/services, not a national census. This note asks what a researcher can conclude from public records without confusing institutional claims, design requirements and measured outcomes.

## Why a data project rather than a directory

AI Watch now treats named systems and institutions as its primary units. Twelve new profiles are joined to six earlier source-backed cases; policy comparisons remain separate supporting material. The reusable contribution is the coding and provenance structure: dated deployment observations, source-qualified institutional roles, procurement records, evaluation settings, typed metrics, all twelve accountability fields and explicit gaps.

This is not a claim to invent AI tracking. The government's [AI Impact Casebooks already cover more than 170 deployed or scalable innovations across India and other countries](https://impact.indiaai.gov.in/events/released-compendium), while the [DCI Social Protection AI Hub already documents Kisan e-Mitra](https://socialprotectionai.org/use-case/IND-001/print). The test of value is whether another researcher can reproduce a distinction or finding and use the linked data, not whether this website has more cards.

## Findings that a flat list would obscure

### A real field study can complicate a headline accuracy claim

The published e-Paarvai study reports 2,619 eyes of 1,407 patients, 96% sensitivity, 25% specificity, 88% accuracy, 92.3% positive predictive value and 57.8% negative predictive value. It recruited people over forty with best-corrected visual acuity below 20/40 at a tertiary eye hospital during January–April 2022 and compared the app with ophthalmologists' slit-lamp examinations. These are study measurements in a selected setting, not a national or current-production benchmark. ([Indian Journal of Ophthalmology study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10538832/))

The data therefore preserves specificity alongside accuracy rather than reduce the result to “88% accurate.” A journalist can ask how false-positive referrals, service capacity and population differences are managed, but cannot infer observed harm or current-version failure from this study alone. ([Study methods and results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10538832/))

The app-store listing separately declares no data collected and an option to request deletion; these are developer disclosures, not findings from an independent live data-flow inspection. The registry keeps them out of the diagnostic-performance table. ([e-Paarvai listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.codevector.cataract_detection_app&hl=en_US))

### Railway requirements are not measured success rates

RDSO's elephant intrusion-detection specification expects 100% primary-event detection and says physically verified false alarms should not exceed 5% of generated alarms per section/month, or the user Railway's defined threshold. These are normative requirements with a particular denominator, not observed false-positive rates or proof of “95% accuracy.” ([RDSO specification](https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/2024-10-23-Signed%20specification%20EIDS.pdf))

The government separately reported 141 route kilometres of operational Northeast Frontier Railway deployment in February 2026 and sanctioned further works. Working coverage and sanctioned coverage stay separate; neither supplies a reviewed acceptance-test distribution. ([Railway parliamentary explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2223196&reg=48&lang=2))

October reporting attributes 255 elephants saved during January–September 2026 to a combination of AI alerts, Plan Bee, speed restrictions and vigilance. The division figures in the same article total 257, not 255, so the inconsistency is retained rather than reconciled by invention. Neither figure estimates an AI-only causal effect. ([Indian Express reporting](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/ai-alerts-plan-bee-railways-saved-255-elephants-northeast-10906293/))

### Funding and supplier evidence have different meanings

Bharat-VISTAAR's Rs 150 crore is an announced allocation, not verified disbursement or an executed vendor award. Project Insight's July 2016 statement announces a signed L & T Infotech contract but provides no reviewed executed agreement or current continuation evidence here. Southern Railway's 7.85 plus 11.14 crore IDS works are a news-reported regional award, not a verified nationwide supplier contract or expenditure figure. These enter three different procurement record types, not a pooled “AI spending” total. ([Bharat-VISTAAR reply](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2239788&reg=3&lang=1), [Project Insight announcement](https://pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?relid=147283), [Southern Railway contract reporting](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2024/Sep/22/system-being-installed-at-rs-19-cr-to-curb-jumbo-deaths-on-rly-tracks-southern-railway))

The first phase of Insight was expected to go live in May 2017; an expectation is not coded as achieved go-live. Its reporting portal describes INTRAC analytics and compliance processes, but the reviewed descriptions do not establish a specific current AI model. It remains a qualified analytics case rather than being silently counted with explicit model-specific deployments. ([Contract announcement](https://pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?relid=147283), [Insight portal](https://report.insight.gov.in/reporting-webapp/portal/recentArticle))

### Chronology is not necessarily contradiction

Digi Yatra's official May 2026 description says 38 airports, while the August parliamentary answer says 39. The dataset retains both dated observations instead of marking this as a conflict. The reported cumulative usage is more than 10.75 crore uses, not that many unique travellers. ([May statement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2267024&reg=3&lang=1), [August answer](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS273_Y4e9cy.pdf?source=lsapps))

Bharat-VISTAAR's March explanation identifies the February Phase-I launch in Hindi and English, while its undated portal advertises 22+ languages. Later rollout may explain this, but the undated interface is not assigned a date or allowed to silently overwrite the dated phase description. ([March explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2239788&reg=3&lang=1), [Service portal](https://vistaar.da.gov.in/))

### A retention promise needs a component boundary

Digi Yatra's parliamentary answer describes airport data purged within 24 hours of scheduled departure. Foundation terms separately identify a 14-day DYCE transaction log, so “all data is deleted within 24 hours” would overstate the reviewed evidence. Published opt-out and privacy-contact terms do not establish successful remedies in practice. ([Parliamentary answer](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS273_Y4e9cy.pdf?source=lsapps), [Foundation privacy policy](https://digiyatrafoundation.com/privacy-policy/))

The Foundation describes itself as a Section 8 not-for-profit company. Public-service operation is therefore not coded as proof of government ownership, and model-specific architecture remains qualified rather than inferred from the mere presence of facial biometrics. ([Foundation policy](https://digiyatrafoundation.com/privacy-policy/))

### Usage is not quality, and an institutional launch is not an oversight audit

BHASHINI's September statement reports more than 24 million daily inferences and launches CAG-BHASHINI Anuvaad with appropriate human oversight language. These establish the content of the official description, not accuracy rates or records of actual reviewer interventions. Its published terms explicitly call outputs probabilistic and place verification responsibility on users. ([September launch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2317011), [BHASHINI terms](https://bhashini.gov.in/gyankosh?tab=terms-of-use&fromFooter=true))

Kisan e-Mitra's February 2026 description reports more than 93 lakh queries answered as of December 2025; that does not measure successful grievance resolution or eligibility decisions. The parliamentary answer describes separate CPGRAMS tracking and appeals. General scheme grievance mechanisms are not assumed to demonstrate a chatbot-specific error appeal. ([Agriculture backgrounder](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2227914&reg=3&lang=1), [Farmer grievance answer](https://www.sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/185/AU2612_qsk9qb.pdf?source=pqals))

The August answer also reports over 95 lakh queries “resolved” as of 15 July 2025, whereas the later backgrounder gives over 93 lakh “responded to” by December. Both are lower bounds and wording differs, so this is not proof of decreasing use; the registry explicitly preserves the unreconciled definitions rather than build a misleading time series. ([August answer](https://www.sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/185/AU2612_qsk9qb.pdf?source=pqals), [February backgrounder](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2227914&reg=3&lang=1))

### Historical service descriptions cannot certify current operation

IRCTC's 2020–21 annual report describes AskDISHA as an enquiry assistant with CoRover and booking as a future extension. A currently accessible AskDISHA 2.0 interface shows booking support, but an undated UI observation is not an exact rollout date or a tested booking. The latest listed annual-report PDF could not be retrieved in this research round, so it is not claimed as reviewed. ([Exchange-filed annual report](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/1f482507-98ed-4566-8323-ea95fc1f82be.pdf), [AskDISHA interface](https://revamp.irctc.corover.ai/))

The Telangana department identifies PLCS and RTA FEST launches in April 2019 and July 2020. Its government-hosted PLCS case study reports “about 93%” authentication success without a reviewed sample size or current measurement protocol. The historical launch and attributed success claim cannot certify October 2026 availability, error rates or safeguards. ([Department description](https://it.telangana.gov.in/initiatives/electronic-services-delivery/), [PLCS case study](https://indiaai.gov.in/case-study/telangana-government-leveraging-the-power-of-ai-and-ml-for-pensioners))

ADVAIT is officially described as rolled out in 2021 to aid tax officers. Officer-assistance wording does not prove either fully autonomous assessments or a documented model-flag override procedure; this corpus does not establish its current error distribution. ([Parliamentary explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1911271))

### A clinical workflow count is not an algorithm validation

Government-hosted reporting describes BMC's 2021–22 Qure.ai pilot, with around 9,702 people asked to return, 2,376 returning and 704 confirmed TB diagnoses. Follow-up attrition and confirmations are workflow evidence, not sensitivity/specificity, and the reporting does not establish the exact qXR version. The profile uses a descriptive BMC–Qure.ai pilot label rather than inventing a product-version linkage. ([Pilot reporting](https://indiaai.gov.in/news/this-ai-software-detects-tuberculosis-in-asymptomatic-patients-in-the-early-stage))

A retrieved qXR study concerns northeast Nigeria, not this Indian municipal pilot. A NITI-hosted Qure.ai story explicitly disclaims endorsement, certification and validation. Neither is transferred into a BMC independent-evaluation claim. ([Nigeria study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11622919/), [NITI submission disclaimer](https://frontiertech.niti.gov.in/story/ai-driven-tuberculosis-diagnosis-a-transformative-solution-for-indias-healthcare-challenges/))

## Using the six earlier cases without losing their limits

UPSC's scaled May 2026 mobile face authentication is not automatically the application procured in a broader 2024 biometric/AI-CCTV tender. The older retention and physical-verification requirements therefore stay qualified, rather than become claims of current mobile-app behaviour. ([UPSC implementation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2268879&reg=3&lang=1), [2024 tender](https://upsc.gov.in/sites/default/files/Tender-AadhaarQRFaceRecgAI-Corrgn_Eng-180724.pdf))

The six inherited cases preserve their full twelve-dimensional evidence, including experimental SUPACE descriptions, clinical-validation limits and differing policing pilot counts. No paper, tender, vendor account or official announcement becomes an independent operational audit by being placed in a relational table. Use each dossier's original references and uncertainty fields to check the exact statement.

## What is reproducible now

The release includes eighteen system records, linked institutions and observations, 216 accountability assertions, unique selected excerpts with source dates/types, seventeen CSV tables, JSON, SQLite, source snapshots, all eighteen dossiers, research receipts and runnable SQL. The 62-query search log documents this round, including recency checks; it is not evidence that every relevant record was found.

The archive manifest and hashes allow a reader to verify package completeness, run the joins, reproduce corpus totals and retrieve the original evidence behind a claim. They do not establish the truth of an agency statement or restore a blocked document. Unknown fields stay present, and undated evidence is not transformed into a precise operational timeline.

## Honest research-value assessment

This is a usable exploratory research dataset and evidence-coding framework, not proof of national coverage, novel causal findings or user demand. Its strongest concrete contribution is preventing consequential category errors while making the underlying decisions inspectable and reusable. Its weakest parts are purposive sampling, single-analyst coding, incomplete contract/appeal records and limited current-version evaluation evidence.

Do not publish “Indian AI systems lack safeguards” from these data. A defensible pilot question is narrower: which kinds of claims can this selected public evidence establish, and where do versions, settings and operational procedures break the link? Independent second coding, a defined institutional sampling frame and real researcher use are needed before a stronger empirical study. More website features would not substitute for those steps.
