# AI Watch: Elephant Intrusion Detection System

India institution-and-system dataset v1.3.0. Checked 2026-10-05; analyst-coded documentary evidence, not an operational audit. System kind: Public-service system. Research cohort: 1.0.0. Selection: Purposive exploratory seed.

## Owner & purpose

Evidence status: Documented. Indian Railways' acoustic-sensing elephant warning system.

> development of AI-enabled Intrusion Detection System (IDS) for detecting presence of elephants on Railway tracks using Distributed Acoustic Sensors (DAS).
>
> [Railway elephant IDS parliamentary explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2223196&reg=48&lang=2); Selected passage 1: Railway elephant IDS parliamentary explanation.

## Decision role & affected people

Evidence status: Documented. Alerts railway personnel for preventive action; not verified autonomous train control.

> The system is designed to generate alerts for loco pilots, station masters and Control Room about the movement of elephants in proximity of railway tracks, for taking timely preventive action.
>
> [Railway elephant IDS parliamentary explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2223196&reg=48&lang=2); Selected passage 2: Railway elephant IDS parliamentary explanation.

## Deployment & date

Evidence status: Partial. 141 route-km official working extent in February; October outcome reporting is multi-intervention, not independently audited deployment telemetry.
