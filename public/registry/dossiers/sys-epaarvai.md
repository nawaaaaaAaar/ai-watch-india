# AI Watch: e-Paarvai

India institution-and-system dataset v1.1.0. Checked 2026-10-05; analyst-coded documentary evidence, not an operational audit. System kind: Public-service system. Selection: Purposive exploratory seed.

## Owner & purpose

Evidence status: Documented. TNeGA/NHM-developed screening app used by TNSBCS according to government-hosted case.

> The e-Paarvai app is currently being used by the Tamil Nadu State Control Blindness Society (TNSBCS) to screen citizens in more than 30 districts in Tamil Nadu.
>
> [Government-hosted e-Paarvai implementation case](https://abhinavpahal.nic.in/uploads/jiRLj8ssnLClarity%20in%20Governance%20Heralds%20Clear%20Vision.pdf); Selected passage 1: Government-hosted e-Paarvai implementation case.
> NHM in collaboration with TNeGA developed an AI-based Android mobile application
>
> [e-Paarvai TNeGA app listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.codevector.cataract_detection_app&hl=en_US); Selected passage 1: e-Paarvai TNeGA app listing.

## Decision role & affected people

Evidence status: Documented. Field-worker cataract screening/classification supports referral; not autonomous surgery eligibility.

> The AI Model is rolled out using an Android App (e-Paarvai) where the field workers capture the eye of the patient and know whether they have mature cataract, immature cataract, IOL or no cataract.
>
> [Government-hosted e-Paarvai implementation case](https://abhinavpahal.nic.in/uploads/jiRLj8ssnLClarity%20in%20Governance%20Heralds%20Clear%20Vision.pdf); Selected passage 2: Government-hosted e-Paarvai implementation case.

## Evaluation & error rates

Evidence status: Documented. Published field study reports sensitivity 96%, specificity 25% and accuracy 88% on 2,619 eyes/1,407 selected patients; not a general population/current-version benchmark.

> Patients aged >40 years and with a best-corrected visual acuity <20/40 were recruited for the study.
>
> [e-Paarvai cataract field study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10538832/); Selected passage 2: e-Paarvai cataract field study.
> In this prospective diagnostic study conducted between January and April 2022 at a large tertiary-care eye hospital in South India, two screeners were trained to use the app.
>
> [e-Paarvai cataract field study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10538832/); Selected passage 1: e-Paarvai cataract field study.
> The diagnosis of the app was compared with that of trained ophthalmologists based on slit-lamp examinations, the gold standard
>
> [e-Paarvai cataract field study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10538832/); Selected passage 4: e-Paarvai cataract field study.
> The two screeners used the app to screen 2,619 eyes of 1,407 patients. In detecting cataracts, the app showed high sensitivity (96%) but low specificity (25%), an overall accuracy of 88%, a PPV of 92.3%, and an NPV of 57.8%.
>
> [e-Paarvai cataract field study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10538832/); Selected passage 3: e-Paarvai cataract field study.
