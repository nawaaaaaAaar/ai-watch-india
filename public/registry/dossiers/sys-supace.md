# AI Watch: SUPACE research assistance

India institution-and-system dataset v1.1.0. Checked 2026-10-05; analyst-coded documentary evidence, not an operational audit. System kind: Public-service system. Selection: Purposive exploratory seed.

## Owner & purpose

Evidence status: Documented. Supreme Court research-assistance system for identifying precedents and case facts.

> The Supreme Court Portal for Assistance in Court Efficiency (SUPACE) is an AI-based system designed to help identify relevant precedents and understand the factual matrix of cases. SUPACE remains in an experimental stage and is not yet deployed for regular judicial use.
>
> [Official judicial AI backgrounder](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2226283&reg=48&lang=2); SUPACE research-assistance section.
