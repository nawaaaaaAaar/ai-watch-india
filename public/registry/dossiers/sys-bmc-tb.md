# AI Watch: BMC–Qure.ai AI-assisted TB screening pilot

India institution-and-system dataset v1.3.0. Checked 2026-10-05; analyst-coded documentary evidence, not an operational audit. System kind: Public-service system. Research cohort: 1.0.0. Selection: Purposive exploratory seed.

## Owner & purpose

Evidence status: Partial. Government-hosted news attributes a nine-hospital pilot to BMC and Qure.ai; direct signed agency records not reviewed.

> around 1 lakh people underwent AI-powered x-ray scans for diagnosing TB at nine BMC hospitals in 2021 and 2022. BMC, along with qure.ai, a digital start-up company, initiated this study.
>
> [BMC–Qure.ai TB screening pilot reporting](https://indiaai.gov.in/news/this-ai-software-detects-tuberculosis-in-asymptomatic-patients-in-the-early-stage); Selected passage 1: BMC–Qure.ai TB screening pilot reporting.

## Decision role & affected people

Evidence status: Documented. AI-assisted X-ray screening flags presumptive cases for clinical evaluation/sputum confirmation; not sole confirmed diagnosis.

> when around 9,702 were asked to return for the clinical evaluation and sputum collection to confirm the diagnosis, only 2,376 turned up, among which 704 turned out to have confirmed TB diagnosis.
>
> [BMC–Qure.ai TB screening pilot reporting](https://indiaai.gov.in/news/this-ai-software-detects-tuberculosis-in-asymptomatic-patients-in-the-early-stage); Selected passage 3: BMC–Qure.ai TB screening pilot reporting.
