# AI Watch: SUVAS judicial translation

India institution-and-system dataset v1.3.0. Checked 2026-10-05; analyst-coded documentary evidence, not an operational audit. System kind: Public-service system. Research cohort: 1.0.0. Selection: Purposive exploratory seed.

## Owner & purpose

Evidence status: Documented. Supreme Court SUVAS translates judgments and orders to vernacular languages.

> For this, SUVAS (Supreme Court Vidhik Anuvaad Software), an AI-driven translation tool of the Supreme Court, converts English judgments and orders into vernacular languages, enhancing accessibility and regional language use in courts. These translated judgments are hosted on the e-SCR portal, significantly expanding public access.
>
> [Official judicial AI backgrounder](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2226283&reg=48&lang=2); Judgment translation section.

## Decision role & affected people

Evidence status: Documented. Translation supports people reading court records; it is not itself a decision on the merits of a case.

> Supreme Court and High Court AI Translation Committees oversee quality and constitutional accuracy. AI use remains assistive, with translations reviewed within judicial frameworks to support access to justice.

## Funding & contract

Evidence status: Partial. The historical Allahabad notice quotes Re. 1 per English-source word. Circulated scheme guidance describes High Court grants sanctioned by State Governments for translator remuneration; neither is a national software budget or verified expenditure.

> Amounts required for paying reasonable remuneration to the panel of translators shall be from grants sought for by the High Court and sanctioned by the State Government.
>
> [Court translation scheme and Supreme Court AI Committee guidelines](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec03ac73001b1d44f4925449ce09d9f5/uploads/2023/09/2023092013.pdf); Annexure A, guideline 10.
> The remuneration for vetting & correction of the software generated translation of the judgments of this Court will be paid at the rate of Re. 1/- per word of the English version.
>
> [Allahabad translation-vetting notice](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/event_16290_18-09-2023.pdf); Allahabad notice, remuneration.

## Human review & overrides

Evidence status: Documented. Translation committees are described; circulated 2023 scheme guidance requires correctness checks by translators/reviewers. Allahabad's separate certification process is local, not universal. Current implementation across all courts is not audited.

> Supreme Court and High Court AI Translation Committees oversee quality and constitutional accuracy. AI use remains assistive, with translations reviewed within judicial frameworks to support access to justice.
>
> [Official judicial AI backgrounder](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2226283&reg=48&lang=2); Translation committee oversight.
> After due vetting & corrections, the said Vetters shall return the same, certifying the correctness of the translation.
>
> [Allahabad translation-vetting notice](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/event_16290_18-09-2023.pdf); Allahabad notice, condition 2.
> Care should be taken by verifying the correctness of translation through the translators/reviewers (retired or in-service translators or retired judicial officers).
>
> [Court translation scheme and Supreme Court AI Committee guidelines](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec03ac73001b1d44f4925449ce09d9f5/uploads/2023/09/2023092013.pdf); Annexure A, guideline 4.
