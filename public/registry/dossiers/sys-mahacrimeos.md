# AI Watch: MahaCrimeOS investigation support

India institution-and-system dataset v1.1.0. Checked 2026-10-05; analyst-coded documentary evidence, not an operational audit. System kind: Public-service system. Selection: Purposive exploratory seed.

## Owner & purpose

Evidence status: Partial. Vendor material describes MARVEL adapting CyberEye's CrimeOS with Microsoft technology; the government-authorisation instrument was not verified.

> MARVEL worked to adapt CrimeOS AI, developed by CyberEye - Microsoft AI Partner, to finetune it for compliance with state investigation protocols and further configure it in Marathi for easy adoption across the force.
>
> [Microsoft CrimeOS customer story](https://www.microsoft.com/en-in/aifirstmovers/FY26CrimeOS); Customer story, supplier and adaptation.

## Decision role & affected people

Evidence status: Partial. The reported purpose is complaint processing and investigation assistance; legal consequences and decision boundaries were not verified.

> Since April, police there have been using MahaCrimeOS AI, a customized crime investigation platform powered by Microsoft Foundry that helps them process complaints faster and navigate complex data and procedures — all crucial functions for handling cybercrime.
>
> [Microsoft MahaCrimeOS feature](https://news.microsoft.com/source/asia/features/a-race-against-time-maharashtra-police-get-an-ai-copilot-to-fight-cybercrime/); Vendor account, reported pilot use.
