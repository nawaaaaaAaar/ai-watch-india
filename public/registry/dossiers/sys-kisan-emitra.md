# AI Watch: Kisan e-Mitra

India institution-and-system dataset v1.0.0. Checked 2026-10-05; analyst-coded documentary evidence, not an operational audit.

## Owner & purpose

Evidence status: Documented. Agriculture department's PM-KISAN assistance chatbot.

> a voice-based PM-KISAN AI Chatbot (Kisan e-Mitra) was developed.
>
> [Farmer grievance-redress parliamentary answer](https://www.sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/185/AU2612_qsk9qb.pdf?source=pqals); Selected passage 1: Farmer grievance-redress parliamentary answer.

## Decision role & affected people

Evidence status: Documented. Queries and application/eligibility information, not verified autonomous benefit adjudication.

> A pan-India informational chatbot that responds to farmer queries and offers transparency around the application status, eligibility, and other information related to the PM-Kisan scheme.
>
> [SamagraX Kisan e-Mitra developer portfolio](https://samagrax.samagragovernance.in/agriculture/); Selected passage 1: SamagraX Kisan e-Mitra developer portfolio.
> Kisan e-Mitra, launched in 2023, is a voice-enabled, AI-powered chatbot designed to support farmers by answering queries on key government schemes
>
> [AI agriculture backgrounder: Kisan e-Mitra](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2227914&reg=3&lang=1); Selected passage 1: AI agriculture backgrounder: Kisan e-Mitra.
