Railway AI video surveillance expansion
Original: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2249823&reg=3&lang=1
Document date: 2026-04-07
Source type: Government deployment statement
Checked: 2026-10-05

Indian Railways has expanded its Video Surveillance System (VSS) across 1,874 railway stations.

The system is equipped with advanced AI-based Video Analytics for automated detection of events such as intrusion and loitering, along with Facial Recognition Software for real-time identification and monitoring.
