SANJAY is an automated system which integrates the inputs from all ground and aerial battlefield sensors

SANJAY has been indigenously & jointly developed by the Indian Army and Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL)

This system has been developed under the Buy (Indian) category at a cost of Rs 2,402 crore.
