Cumulative savings though use of FRI rose from ₹139.16 crore in August 2025 to ₹5,043.73 crore by August 2026

FRI is developed under DoT's Digital Intelligence Platform (DIP), that classifies mobile numbers into 3 categories namely Medium, High, or Very High risk-based on likelihood of their involvement in financial fraud.

Banks and UPI service providers are already using FRI intelligence to stop or flag transactions involving high and very high risk mobile numbers in real time.
