iOncology.ai is currently undergoing pilot-stage clinical validation and implementation evaluation

The platform, iOncology.ai, has been developed by the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi, and the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC), Pune

iOncology.ai is not yet clinically validated.

It is being evaluated in research mode, with no peer-reviewed evidence yet establishing its diagnostic accuracy, clinical utility or effectiveness.
