Through Friendly Electronic Services of Transport (FEST), launched in July, 2020, the citizens of Telangana can avail numerous services of the Transport Department through a Smartphone

PLCS is the first usecase of RTDAI and has been launched in April, 2019.

RTDAI enables a document-less, presence-less governance mechanism using Big Data, AI, ML, etc.

Except for services where a driving test or physical inspection of the vehicle is required, all services would be offered in a contactless and presence-less manner through FEST.
