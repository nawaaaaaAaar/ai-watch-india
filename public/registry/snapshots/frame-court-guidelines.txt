AI-based transcription tools are being used in the Supreme Court to automatically capture and convert oral arguments, particularly before Constitution Benches, into real-time text displayed on courtroom screens.

These live transcripts are then uploaded to the Court’s website, enabling lawyers, litigants, researchers, and the wider public to access an authoritative and contemporaneous record of hearings
