The AI Model is rolled out using an Android App (e-Paarvai) where the field workers capture the eye of the patient and know whether they have mature cataract, immature cataract, IOL or no cataract.

The e-Paarvai app is currently being used by the Tamil Nadu State Control Blindness Society (TNSBCS) to screen citizens in more than 30 districts in Tamil Nadu.
