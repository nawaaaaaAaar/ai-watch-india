# CPGRAMS Intelligent Grievance Management System (IGMS): implementation and procurement evidence

Checked 2026-10-05. Existing registry family `sys-igms`; baseline values unchanged.

## What changes

Manual closure, isolated LLM processing and legacy-data training provisions become inspectable. Official 2022 and September 2023 launch descriptions remain unresolved; NextGen was still described as under development in August 2026.

Successful NextGen bidder/contract, acceptance/go-live record, classifier performance and AI-specific appeals remain unverified; general portal/privacy routes are context, not model-specific safeguards.
