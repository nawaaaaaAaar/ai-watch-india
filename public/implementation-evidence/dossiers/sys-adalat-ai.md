### Implementation

Primary records reviewed; strength/stage varies. Next record: Dated acceptance, commissioning, training-completion or go-live records for the named scope.

The circular reports that a pilot began on 03.11.2025 in all Civil Judge (Junior Division) Courts in Chittoor, East Godavari, Guntur, and Srikakulam. Judicial officers in the remaining Units were permitted to use the platform voluntarily. The circular says the pilot's implementation was monitored, but gives no results or usage figures. ([Mandatory use of the Adalat.AI Platform in the District Judiciary of Andhra Pradesh](https://aphc.gov.in/docs/notification_1785225296_0.pdf)).

Strength: First-party reported activity or outcome; type: Official reported activity; event date: 2025-11-03. Independent outcome verification: no.

> the use of Adalat.AI Platform was introduced with effect from 03.11.2025 in all Courts of Civil Judges (Junior Division)

Paragraph 2. [Original text](https://aphc.gov.in/docs/notification_1785225296_0.pdf).

Limit: This is the High Court's account of the pilot; the circular does not give usage totals, performance results, or independent evaluation.
