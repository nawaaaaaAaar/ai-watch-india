## UPSC face authentication: UPSC face authentication: decision-accountability case

Collection: service. Comparison: System claim to accountability evidence. Type: Public-service case. Actor/class: Public examinations. Earlier context: 4 June release, implementation and developer.

Status: Official deployment reported.

### Textual observation

UPSC reports nationwide mobile face authentication in May 2026; the current application is described as developed with NeGD support. [4 June release, implementation and developer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2268879&reg=3&lang=1) [4 October release, examination reform](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2317336&reg=48&lang=1)

Earlier excerpt (SYSTEM RECORD, as extracted):

> The face authentication application has been developed and implemented by UPSC with technical support from the National e-Governance Division (NeGD) of Ministry of Electronics and IT.

Later excerpt (SUPPORTING RECORD, as printed, as extracted):

> Following a pilot at select centres in Gurugram during the NDA & NA (II) and CDS (II) examinations in September 2025, the system was implemented at scale for the Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination on 24 May 2026, covering approximately 5.50 lakh candidates.

### Analyst interpretation

Identity verification can affect entry to an examination. Throughput, coverage and an older tender are not a measured false-rejection rate or proof that a rejected candidate has a documented fallback route.

### Limits and follow-up

Do not equate the 2024 biometric/CCTV procurement package with the 2026 in-house mobile application. Tender requirements are not current deployed behaviour. No candidate case or device inspection was undertaken.

Reporting question: Which decision can this system influence, what evidence supports its use, and how can a person obtain human review?

Evidence status: Partial.

The older tender requires physical photo verification. The current mismatch/technical-failure SOP was not verified.

#### Privacy & retention

Evidence status: Partial.

The older tender specifies encrypted cloud holding for at least one year or 30 days after final results, whichever is later. Applying it to the current mobile system is not established.

[UPSC biometric/AI tender specification: 2024 tender clause 6.1.12](https://upsc.gov.in/sites/default/files/Tender-AadhaarQRFaceRecgAI-Corrgn_Eng-180724.pdf)

> 6.1.12 After the completion of the entire process as per the scope of work, the Service Provider will hold the data on its Secured Cloud Server with 256-bit encryption for a minimum period of one (01) year from the date of the examination or 30 days after the declaration of final result of the examination, whichever is later.
