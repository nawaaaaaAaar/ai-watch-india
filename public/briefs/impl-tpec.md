### Textual observation

The TPEC memorandum is dated 13 April; its public announcement is dated 18 April. [Part 2.6; printed p. 35](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc2025115685601.pdf) [Office memorandum, p. 1](https://d12aarmt01l54a.cloudfront.net/cms/files/constitution-of-tpec/1776447206.pdf)

> The TPEC’s primary goal is to provide expertise to the AI Governance Group (AIGG) and enable it to perform its functions effectively.

Later excerpt (REVIEWED IMPLEMENTATION RECORD, as printed, as extracted):

> Subject: - Constitution of Technology and Policy Expert Committee (TPEC).-reg. The undersigned is directed to convey constitution olTechnology and Policy Expert Committee (TPEC).

### Analyst interpretation

The six-seat composition combines the MeitY secretary, two academic seats and representatives of NASSCOM, DSCI and MAIT. An advisory remit is not a statutory licence to approve or ban every AI system.

### Limits and follow-up

This verifies the order, not completion of work, attendance or the currently serving individuals. No inference about legitimacy follows solely from the composition. OCR text needs checking against the PDF.

Reporting question: What risk assessments or recommendations has TPEC delivered, and how are expertise and conflicts managed?
