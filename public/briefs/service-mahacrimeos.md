## MahaCrimeOS investigation support: MahaCrimeOS investigation support: decision-accountability case

Collection: service. Comparison: System claim to accountability evidence. Type: Public-service case. Actor/class: State policing. Earlier context: Customer story, supplier and adaptation.

Status: Vendor/reporting-supported pilot; scale unverified.

### Textual observation

Vendor accounts and reporting describe a Nagpur pilot and announced statewide expansion, with inconsistent 23/25-station counts in the reviewed material. [Customer story, supplier and adaptation](https://www.microsoft.com/en-in/aifirstmovers/FY26CrimeOS) [Vendor account, reported pilot use](https://news.microsoft.com/source/asia/features/a-race-against-time-maharashtra-police-get-an-ai-copilot-to-fight-cybercrime/)

Earlier excerpt (SYSTEM RECORD, as extracted):

> MARVEL worked to adapt CrimeOS AI, developed by CyberEye - Microsoft AI Partner, to finetune it for compliance with state investigation protocols and further configure it in Marathi for easy adoption across the force.

Later excerpt (SUPPORTING RECORD, as printed, as extracted):

> Since April, police there have been using MahaCrimeOS AI, a customized crime investigation platform powered by Microsoft Foundry that helps them process complaints faster and navigate complex data and procedures — all crucial functions for handling cybercrime.

### Analyst interpretation

Investigation support can shape what an officer sees and pursues, but claims of speed or statewide scale need operational records. A company story and a repeating news report are not independent performance audits.

### Limits and follow-up

The reviewed corpus did not establish a government procurement award, station-wise completed rollout, performance audit or tool-specific redress procedure. Count differences may reflect dates/denominators; do not choose a convenient number.

Reporting question: Which decision can this system influence, what evidence supports its use, and how can a person obtain human review?
