## AIGEG: A constitution order exists; policy delivery needs separate evidence

Collection: impl. Comparison: Recommendation to implementation evidence. Type: Evidence checkpoint. Actor/class: IndiaAI, MeitY and oversight bodies. Earlier context: Part 2.6; printed p. 34.

Status: Formal constitution documented.

### Textual observation

A 13 April memorandum constitutes AIGEG; the public announcement follows on 16 April. [Part 2.6; printed p. 34](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc2025115685601.pdf) [Office memorandum, p. 1](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/04/43a4ec455c26b0f061cc7cca98770a45.pdf)

Earlier excerpt (POLICY RECOMMENDATION, as extracted):

> The Committee recommends the creation of an AI Governance Group (AIGG) to develop and oversee India’s position and strategy on AI governance.

Later excerpt (REVIEWED IMPLEMENTATION RECORD, as printed, as extracted):

> Subject: - Constitution of AI Governance and Economic Group (AIGEG) -reg. The undersigned is directed to convey constitution olAI Governance and Economic Group (AIGEG).

### Analyst interpretation

The recommendation has a formal institutional response, with a ten-seat composition and terms of reference. Constitution is not evidence that the group has met, issued decisions or delivered its labour-transition roadmap.

### Limits and follow-up

The reviewed order and announcement do not establish meeting activity or outcomes. OCR errors are visible in the order snapshot; use the original for quoting names and exact wording.

Reporting question: What agendas, minutes, guidelines and deploy/pilot/defer decisions has AIGEG issued?
