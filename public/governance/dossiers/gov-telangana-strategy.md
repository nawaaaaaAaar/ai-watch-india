## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: The strategy proposes a Telangana AI Advisory Council for strategic guidance, supported by implementation wings. It says government departments will appoint AI nodal officers; these are stated plans, not established statutory duties.

Force: Strategy commitment; no statutory obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> All government departments will also appoint an AI nodal officer to drive interventions and outcomes outlined by the AI Advisory Council.

p. 13, AI Direction Setting. [Read the original](https://it.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/AI-Powered-Telangana-Strategy-Document-and-Implementation-Roadmap.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: The strategy describes baseline and end-line assessments to measure the productivity impact of training government officials. It also proposes pilots to assess solutions before scaling them, but the text does not verify that these evaluations occurred.

Force: Strategy commitment; no statutory obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Baseline and end-line productivity assessments will be conducted to measure the program’s impact and track the impact of AI on productivity enhancement.

p. 19, AI for Efficiency. [Read the original](https://it.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/AI-Powered-Telangana-Strategy-Document-and-Implementation-Roadmap.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: The proposed PoC-to-scale process would move solutions that prove effective in pilots to full-scale products through RFPs. This is a planned pathway, not a binding vendor-selection rule.

Force: Strategy commitment; no statutory obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Scale solutions for successful pilots via RfPs

p. 18, Fig. (6), PoC to Scale Journey. [Read the original](https://it.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/AI-Powered-Telangana-Strategy-Document-and-Implementation-Roadmap.pdf).

### Redress

Context / topic, not a concrete control: The strategy identifies AI-enhanced T-App services and grievance redressal as a potential use case. It does not specify a redress procedure, channel, or enforceable remedy.

Force: Strategy commitment; no statutory obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> AI enhanced versions of T-App for more proactive & easy delivery of schemes and services and grievance redressal

p. 17, Fig. (5), AI-powered e-Gov use-cases. [Read the original](https://it.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/AI-Powered-Telangana-Strategy-Document-and-Implementation-Roadmap.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: The strategy proposes tracking dataset use in AI model development and publishing dashboards about impact. It also describes data-sharing safeguards and responsible-AI guidance, but these are planned measures and the text does not verify implementation.

Force: Strategy commitment; no statutory obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The state will track the utilisation of these datasets in AI model development, with public dashboards providing transparency on their impact and contribution to societal development.

p. 23, TGDex: Telangana Data Exchange. [Read the original](https://it.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/AI-Powered-Telangana-Strategy-Document-and-Implementation-Roadmap.pdf).
