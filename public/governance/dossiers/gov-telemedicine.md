## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: The Board of Governors may issue directions, advisories, and clarifications and modify the drug lists. Amendments to the guidelines require prior Central Government approval.

Force: Professional guidance text; current enforcement and later changes not audited. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The Board of Governors in super-session of the Medical Council of India may issue necessary directions or advisories or clarifications in regard to these Guidelines, as required.

p. 34, §6.2. [Read the original](https://health.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-12/Telemedicine-practice-guidelines.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: RMPs are to judge whether telemedicine is appropriate, gather sufficient patient information, and refer for in-person examination when needed. A program-level evaluation or monitoring framework is not located in the reviewed text; this is not proof of absence.

Force: Professional guidance text; current enforcement and later changes not audited. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The Registered Medical Practitioners should exercise their professional judgment to decide whether a telemedicine consultation is appropriate in a given situation or an in-person consultation is needed in the interest of the patient.

p. 16, §3.1.1. [Read the original](https://health.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-12/Telemedicine-practice-guidelines.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: Technology platforms providing telemedicine must have a mechanism to address customer queries or grievances. No broader or separate patient redress process is located in the reviewed text.

Force: Professional guidance text; current enforcement and later changes not audited. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Technology Platform must ensure that there is a proper mechanism in place to address any queries or grievances that the end-customer may have

p. 33, §5.6. [Read the original](https://health.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-12/Telemedicine-practice-guidelines.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: RMPs must uphold medical ethics, privacy, and confidentiality, and retain consultation records, patient materials, and prescription records. Platforms must verify listed RMP credentials and report non-compliance; AI/ML platforms may not counsel patients or prescribe medicines.

Force: Professional guidance text; current enforcement and later changes not audited. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Principles of medical ethics, including professional norms for protecting patient privacy and confidentiality as per IMC Act shall be binding and must be upheld and practiced.

p. 23, §3.7.1.1. [Read the original](https://health.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-12/Telemedicine-practice-guidelines.pdf).
