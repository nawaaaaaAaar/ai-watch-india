## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: Stock exchanges and depositories must compile intermediaries’ reports and submit consolidated quarterly reports to SEBI. They must also amend relevant rules, notify members or participants, and report implementation status to SEBI.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Stock Exchanges and Depositories have to consolidate and compile a report, on AI / ML applications and systems reported by registered Stock Brokers / Depository Participants

Page 2, paragraph 7. [Read the original](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jan-2019/1546611637895.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: The reporting form asks for a system description, safeguards against abnormal behavior, cyber-control information, and whether the system is included in a system audit. These are reporting fields; the text does not establish a separate substantive evaluation standard.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.
