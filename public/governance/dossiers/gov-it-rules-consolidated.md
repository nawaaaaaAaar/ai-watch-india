## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: Part III establishes a three-tier structure of publisher self-regulation, publisher-body self-regulation, and Central Government oversight. The Ministry establishes an Inter-Departmental Committee to hear specified grievances and the Review Committee meets at least every two months to review blocking directions.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The Ministry shall co-ordinate and facilitate the adherence to the Code of Ethics by publishers and self regulating bodies, develop an Oversight Mechanism

Rule 13(1). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/0b576f2071694b52e4cd6bb1b6dfab1e.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: For specified significant social media intermediary tools, the rules require review of accuracy, fairness, bias, discrimination, privacy, and security impacts. Online real-money games are verified against stated criteria, including no wagering on an outcome and compliance with specified rules and frameworks.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> the review of automated tools under this sub-rule shall evaluate the automated tools having regard to the accuracy and fairness of such tools

Rule 4(4). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/0b576f2071694b52e4cd6bb1b6dfab1e.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: An intermediary’s Grievance Officer must acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and generally resolve them within seven days; specified intimate-image and impersonation complaints have a two-hour action requirement. The rules also provide for appeals to Grievance Appellate Committees.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> acknowledge the complaint within twenty-four hours and resolve such complaint within a period of 4[seven days]

Rule 3(2)(a)(i). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/0b576f2071694b52e4cd6bb1b6dfab1e.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Specified intermediaries must appoint compliance and grievance personnel and publish monthly compliance reports. The rules also provide for preservation of removed information and associated records for 180 days, and public monthly disclosure of grievances and their disposition by publishers and self-regulating bodies.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> preserve such information and associated records for one hundred and eighty days for investigation purposes

Rule 3(1)(g). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/0b576f2071694b52e4cd6bb1b6dfab1e.pdf).
