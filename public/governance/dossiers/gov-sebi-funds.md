## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: Registered mutual funds using covered AI/ML systems must submit the reporting form to AMFI quarterly within 15 calendar days after each quarter. AMFI must consolidate the reports and submit them to SEBI within 30 calendar days after the quarter, while maintaining confidentiality.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> AMFI shall consolidate the information on AI / ML applications and systems reported by Mutual Funds on quarterly basis and submit to SEBI

Page 2, paragraph 7; confidentiality requirement at page 3, paragraph 8. [Read the original](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/may-2019/1557381806499.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: The reporting form asks whether the AI/ML system is within the scope of a system audit, if applicable, and whether the audit contains adverse comments. These are reporting questions; the text does not establish a separate evaluation standard or audit methodology.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.
