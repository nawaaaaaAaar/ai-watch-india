## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: Proposes an independent, multidisciplinary Council for Ethics and Technology to advise government, support coordination with sectoral regulators and develop model guidance. This is a proposal in the document; its establishment is not verified.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> an independent, multi-disciplinary and highly participatory entity

§3.48, printed p. 21. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-08/Part2-Responsible-AI-12082021.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: Recommends assessing the sociotechnical system, including direct and indirect impacts, and identifies sandboxing and controlled deployments as ways to examine uncertain risks. These are proposed approaches, not verified implementation requirements.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The assessment of risk must also take both the direct and indirect impact of the system into consideration and may be done through policy instruments like sandboxing.

§3.8, printed p. 13. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-08/Part2-Responsible-AI-12082021.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: Recommends institutionalizing responsible AI practices in public-sector procurement and developing model procurement mechanisms and documents. The text makes recommendations; it does not establish an operative procurement mandate.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> By mandating the institutionalization of responsible AI practices in public sector procurement, the Government could create a demand for such practices

§4.1.7, printed p. 27. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-08/Part2-Responsible-AI-12082021.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: The document says supporting structures for grievance redressal may be needed for self-regulation to work, and recommends informing stakeholders about grievance mechanisms. It does not set out a specific redress procedure.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Supporting structures to enable accountability, transparency and grievance redressal may be required for self-regulation to be effective.

§3.9, printed p. 13. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-08/Part2-Responsible-AI-12082021.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Identifies data ownership, informed consent, confidentiality and security as matters relevant to identifying liability, and recommends addressing liability in procurement documents. These are proposed research and policy considerations, not established obligations.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Policies on data ownership involving physical safety, informed consent, confidentiality, and security would be beneficial for identifying liabilities.

§3.23, printed p. 16. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-08/Part2-Responsible-AI-12082021.pdf).
