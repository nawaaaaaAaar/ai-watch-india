## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: A task force comprising market infrastructure institutions, QRTAs, QREs and other stakeholders is constituted to coordinate cybersecurity risk mitigation and information sharing. MIIs and other regulated entities are directed to seek guidance from their IT committees on risks from AI-led vulnerability detection models.

Force: Regulatory circular wording distinguished from recommendations; addressee-specific implementation not verified. Implementation independently verified: no.

> MIIs and other Regulated Entities shall seek guidance from their respective IT committees

Annexure-A, p. 7, §10. [Read the original](https://cybersuraksha-ai.sebi.gov.in/documents/48365173/0/Advisory+on+AI.pdf/0ccb30be-015d-f7dc-2b28-8741bf260556?t=1786862751961).
