## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: The paper proposes ethics councils at COREs and ICTAIs to define standard practices and monitor their adoption. This is a recommendation, not an established oversight requirement.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Set up a consortium of Ethics Councils at each CORE and ICTAI to define the standard practices and monitor their adoption.

p. 93, Actions for the Government. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-03/National-Strategy-for-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: The proposed CSTS would monitor consumer-level impacts using social indices and recommend modifications; the paper also suggests considering an AI Readiness Index. These are proposed evaluation measures.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> to monitor the impact of the AI technologies developed at the consumer level through social indices and recommend necessary modifications

p. 55, Research, Centre for Studies on Technological Sustainability. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-03/National-Strategy-for-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf).

### Procurement

Context / topic, not a concrete control: A deployable-model marketplace is proposed to connect solution seekers and providers. The text does not establish mandatory vendor-selection or procurement criteria; marketplace proposals are not binding selection rules.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The model marketplace will attract enterprises which can post problems which they are seeking to be solved using AI tools.

p. 81, Deployable Model Marketplace. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-03/National-Strategy-for-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf).
