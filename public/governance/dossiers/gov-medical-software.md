## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: The document describes licensing authorities under the MDR-2017: manufacturing licences for Class A and B are assigned to the State Licensing Authority, while Class C and D and import licences are assigned to the Central Licensing Authority. It also says the risk class is confirmed by CDSCO on review; this is guidance about the regulatory framework, not verification of implementation.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

### Evaluation

Textual provision: The guidance calls for technical and clinical performance evidence, including verification and validation; it says justification is required when models are trained or validated outside India. These are stated submission and evaluation expectations, not evidence that any particular product has been evaluated.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Where models are trained or validated in non-Indian settings, justification shall be provided for applicability to Indian clinical environments.

Printed page 49, §12.4.2(G). [Read the original](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/Guidance-document-on-Medical-Device-Software-under-MDR-2017.pdf).
