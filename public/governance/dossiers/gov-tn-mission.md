## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: The mission proposal provides for periodic review of mission activities by the Chief Minister. The order directs TNeGA’s Director/Chief Executive Officer to implement the mission and report periodically to Government.

Force: Administrative or policy commitment; not proof of activity or outcomes. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The activities of Tamil Nadu Artificial Intelligence Mission shall be reviewed periodically by the Honourable Chief Minister

Annexed mission proposal, p. 9. [Read the original](https://cms.tn.gov.in/cms_migrated/document/GO/it_e_25_2024_Ms.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: The order requires periodic progress reports to Government. The proposal also describes monitoring and evaluation of capacity-building initiatives, but neither text establishes that evaluation has occurred.

Force: Administrative or policy commitment; not proof of activity or outcomes. Implementation independently verified: no.

> send periodical progress Report to Government.

Government Order, p. 5, direction to Director of e-Governance/TNeGA. [Read the original](https://cms.tn.gov.in/cms_migrated/document/GO/it_e_25_2024_Ms.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: The proposal specifies tendering through QCBS for industry engagement under the Tamil Nadu Transparency in Tenders Act. The order states that in-house development and/or procurement for individual projects follows Governing Council approval; it describes initial ELCOT funding and reimbursement by Government.

Force: Administrative or policy commitment; not proof of activity or outcomes. Implementation independently verified: no.

> the Request for Proposals (RFP) will be floated through Tender(QCBS) mode

Annexed mission proposal, §5.5.2, p. 30. [Read the original](https://cms.tn.gov.in/cms_migrated/document/GO/it_e_25_2024_Ms.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: The mission document identifies sharing Government data as needed, subject to data-protection and data-sharing laws, as an objective. It also identifies ethical and responsible AI guidelines addressing bias, transparency, accountability, and privacy; the text does not verify implementation of these provisions.

Force: Administrative or policy commitment; not proof of activity or outcomes. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Share Government data for implementing the Al ideas -as needed ensuring compliance to data protection and data sharing acts, on force.

Government Order, p. 2, mission objectives. [Read the original](https://cms.tn.gov.in/cms_migrated/document/GO/it_e_25_2024_Ms.pdf).
