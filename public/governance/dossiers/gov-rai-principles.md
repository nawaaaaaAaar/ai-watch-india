## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: The self-assessment guide suggests review by an ethical committee before deployment and ongoing monitoring. These are guide recommendations, not established mandatory oversight requirements.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Organize a review with the ethical committee to assess performance, functioning, various risk mitigation strategies to ensure safe and reliable deployment

Appendix 1, printed p. 49. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-02/Responsible-AI-22022021.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: The self-assessment guide suggests evaluating bias by documenting error rates across sub-populations and testing difficult cases against stated fairness goals. These are recommendations in the guide.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Calculate and document error rates for different sub-population groups and evaluate if the performance is in line with fairness goals set for the system

Appendix 1, printed p. 48. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-02/Responsible-AI-22022021.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: The guide suggests recording use-case fairness goals in procurement documents. It also suggests specifying vendor roles and responsibilities; the text does not establish mandatory vendor-selection criteria.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Define the fairness goals with respect to the use case, in terms of the social cost of inclusion/ exclusion, in the procurement document.

Appendix 1, printed p. 46. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-02/Responsible-AI-22022021.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: A recommended principle calls for accessible and affordable grievance redress where an adverse decision occurs. This is not evidence of a universally established or implemented grievance process.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> In case of an adverse decision, appropriate grievance redressal mechanism should be designed in a manner affordable and accessible to everyone irrespective of their background.

Principle 3, printed p. 41. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-02/Responsible-AI-22022021.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: The principles recommend stakeholder responsibility, risk and impact assessments, auditing and grievance mechanisms. They also recommend privacy and security safeguards for data used in training, including limiting access to authorised parties; these are recommendations, not verified implementation requirements.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Stakeholders should conduct risk and impact assessments to evaluate direct and indirect potential impact of AI systems on end- users

Principle 6, printed p. 41. [Read the original](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-02/Responsible-AI-22022021.pdf).
