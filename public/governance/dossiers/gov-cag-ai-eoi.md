### Oversight

Force: EOI requirements and commissioning intent; not an executed contract or enforced controls. Implementation independently verified: no.

> the agent assists but does not sanction; all final decisions remain with designated officers.

Force: EOI requirements and commissioning intent; not an executed contract or enforced controls. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The POC score will constitute 30% of the overall technical evaluation score in the RFP.

Textual provision: The proposed design calls for logged, reproducible, explainable agent operations and for data, models, and processing to remain within institutionally controlled, India-based infrastructure. These are stated procurement requirements, not implementation verification.
