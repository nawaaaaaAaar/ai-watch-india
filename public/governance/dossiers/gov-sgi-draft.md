### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The draft requires prominent labels or permanent unique metadata or identifiers on covered synthetically generated information and bars intermediaries from enabling their modification, suppression, or removal. For significant social media intermediaries, it also deems due diligence failed in specified cases of knowingly permitting, promoting, or failing to act on non-compliant information.

Force: Historical text; not used to infer current duties. Implementation independently verified: no.

> the intermediary under clause (a) shall not enable the modification, suppression or removal of such label, permanent unique metadata or identifier

Draft rule 3(3)(b). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/9de47fb06522b9e40a61e4731bc7de51.pdf).
