## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: The policy provides for a Safe & Ethical AI Monitoring Committee, headed by the Chief Secretary, to monitor implementation of the guidelines. This is a stated oversight mechanism, not evidence that it operated.

Force: Policy commitment; no independent compliance verification. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Effective implementation of the guidelines shall be monitored by Safe & Ethical AI Monitoring committee headed by chief secretary

Printed p. 37, §9. [Read the original](https://it.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-06/TN_Safe_Ethical_AI_policy_2020.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: The policy states that the State will use the TAM-DEF framework and DEEP-MAX scorecard to evaluate AI systems before public rollout. It also describes proposed score parameters and score updates.

Force: Policy commitment; no independent compliance verification. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The Tamil Nadu Government will use the Six-Dimensional TAM-DEF Framework along with DEEP-MAX Scorecard for evaluating all AI Systems before public roll out

Printed p. 23, §6. [Read the original](https://it.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-06/TN_Safe_Ethical_AI_policy_2020.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: The procurement guidelines say agencies would have to follow the listed steps, including consultation with CEET and evaluation against DEEP-MAX. Some specific directions are framed as requests, recommendations, or encouragement; the text does not establish actual compliance.

Force: Policy commitment; no independent compliance verification. Implementation independently verified: no.

> All Tamil Nadu Government Procuring Agencies would have to follow the following guidelines before procuring any AI Solution/System

Printed p. 35, §8. [Read the original](https://it.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-06/TN_Safe_Ethical_AI_policy_2020.pdf).
