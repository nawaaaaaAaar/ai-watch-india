## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: UIDAI may audit requesting entities, ASAs, and OVSEs itself or through appointed audit agencies. Entities must cooperate and bear audit costs; UIDAI may require clarification or correction of identified deficiencies.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The Authority may undertake audit of the operations, infrastructure, systems and procedures, of requesting entities

### Redress

Textual provision: Authentication logs may be disclosed for grievance redressal and dispute resolution, subject to the stated restrictions. No general grievance channel or complaint procedure is located in the reviewed text.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The requesting entity shall not share the authentication logs with any person other than the concerned Aadhaar number holder upon his/her request or for grievance redressal and resolution of disputes

Regulation 18(4). [Read the original](https://uidai.gov.in/images/The_Aadhaar_Authentication_and_Offline_Verifications_Regulations_2021-_Clean_copy-30122025.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Requesting entities have specified operational, security, audit, and incident-reporting duties. The text prohibits retaining core biometric information and sets rules for authentication logs, including retention and access limits.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> the core biometric information collected from the Aadhaar number holder is not stored, shared or published for any purpose whatsoever

Regulation 17(1)(a). [Read the original](https://uidai.gov.in/images/The_Aadhaar_Authentication_and_Offline_Verifications_Regulations_2021-_Clean_copy-30122025.pdf).
