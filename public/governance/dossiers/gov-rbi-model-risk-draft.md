## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: Draft principle: the Board should oversee the model risk management framework (MRMF), including its approval and review, risk appetite and policies. The Risk Management Committee of the Board (RMCB) should oversee implementation and specified higher-risk model decisions.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The Board should be responsible for the oversight of an RE’s MRMF

p. 6, §11. [Read the original](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/Content/PDFs/DRAFTGUIDANCE24062026FF12A4FF7BC84E8887009D5C5365F8BF.PDF).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: Draft principles call for independent validation of all models before and after deployment, following modification, on triggers and periodically. Validation is to assess inputs, soundness, performance and alignment with intended use; ongoing performance testing is also addressed.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> An RE should ensure that all models, including third-party models, are subject to independent validation by the RE.

p. 10, §29; ongoing testing: p. 8, §16. [Read the original](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/Content/PDFs/DRAFTGUIDANCE24062026FF12A4FF7BC84E8887009D5C5365F8BF.PDF).

### Procurement

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: Draft principles say an RE should conduct due diligence before acquiring or using a third-party model, including on provider credibility, methodological soundness, limitations and data quality. They also describe contractual documentation, audit-right and continuity/exit provisions; the text does not establish a mandatory vendor-selection regime.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Prior to acquisition or use of a third-party model, an RE should undertake due diligence

p. 12, §47; contractual terms: p. 12, §48. [Read the original](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/Content/PDFs/DRAFTGUIDANCE24062026FF12A4FF7BC84E8887009D5C5365F8BF.PDF).

### Redress

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The draft says an RE should not use a model that harms consumers and that its grievance redressal mechanism should address grievances arising from consumer-facing models. This does not establish any additional generic grievance pathway.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Its grievance redressal mechanism should also address grievances arising from consumer facing models used by it.

p. 9, §25. [Read the original](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/Content/PDFs/DRAFTGUIDANCE24062026FF12A4FF7BC84E8887009D5C5365F8BF.PDF).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The draft states that an RE is accountable for outcomes of all models it uses, including third-party models. It also says development data, whether empirical or synthetic, should follow the RE’s data-governance processes; these are draft principles, not verified final obligations.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> An RE is accountable for the outcomes of all models used by it

p. 6, §8; data governance: p. 10, §28. [Read the original](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/Content/PDFs/DRAFTGUIDANCE24062026FF12A4FF7BC84E8887009D5C5365F8BF.PDF).
