## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: Delegated procurement authorities are responsible for efficiency, economy and transparency. Procurement decisions must record the considerations supporting them.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Every authority delegated with the financial powers of procuring goods in public interest shall have the responsibility and accountability to bring efficiency, economy, and transparency

Printed p. 34, Rule 144. [Read the original](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s316026d60ff9b54410b3435b403afd226/uploads/2024/08/202408121698012541.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: Bids must be evaluated against the conditions in the bidding documents, without introducing new evaluation conditions. The text also sets out evaluation methods for consultancy proposals.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Bids received should be evaluated in terms of the conditions already incorporated in the bidding documents; No new condition which was not incorporated in the bidding documents should be brought in for evaluation of the

Printed p. 46, Rule 173(xii). [Read the original](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s316026d60ff9b54410b3435b403afd226/uploads/2024/08/202408121698012541.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: Common-use goods and services available on GeM must be procured through GeM, subject to the specified procedures and thresholds. The rules also establish general requirements for fair, transparent and competitive procurement.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The Procurement of Goods and Services by Ministries or Departments will be mandatory for Goods or Services available on GeM.

Printed p. 35, Rule 149. [Read the original](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s316026d60ff9b54410b3435b403afd226/uploads/2024/08/202408121698012541.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: Bidding documents must allow a bidder to question bidding conditions, the bidding process or rejection of its bid. The text also requires reasons for rejecting a tender or not issuing a tender document to be disclosed when the bidder enquires.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Suitable provision should be kept in the bidding document to enable a bidder to question the bidding conditions, bidding process and/ or rejection of its bid.

Printed p. 45, Rule 173(iv). [Read the original](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s316026d60ff9b54410b3435b403afd226/uploads/2024/08/202408121698012541.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Tender enquiries, corrigenda and bid-award details must be published on the Central Public Procurement Portal, subject to stated confidentiality exceptions. The rules also require procurement decisions to be recorded and provide a code of integrity, including conflict-of-interest disclosure.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> It is mandatory for all Ministries/ Departments of the Central Government, their attached and Subordinate Offices and Autonomous /Statutory Bodies to publish their tender enquiries

Printed p. 38, Rule 159(i). [Read the original](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s316026d60ff9b54410b3435b403afd226/uploads/2024/08/202408121698012541.pdf).
