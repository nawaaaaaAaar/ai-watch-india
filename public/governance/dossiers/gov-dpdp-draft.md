## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The draft gives the Board powers over Consent Manager registration, including the ability to address non-adherence and, after a hearing, suspend or cancel registration. It also provides for the Board to function as a digital office.

Force: Historical text; not used to infer current duties. Implementation independently verified: no.

> suspend or cancel the registration of such Consent Manager

Rule 4(5), Gazette p. 29. [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/02/f8a8e97a91091543fe19139cac7514a1.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: A Significant Data Fiduciary must undertake a Data Protection Impact Assessment and audit every twelve months and have the assessor submit a report of significant observations to the Board. It must also conduct due diligence on algorithmic software for risks to Data Principal rights.

Force: Historical text; not used to infer current duties. Implementation independently verified: no.

> undertake a Data Protection Impact Assessment and an audit to ensure effective observance of the provisions of this Act and the rules made thereunder

Rule 12(1), Gazette p. 34. [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/02/f8a8e97a91091543fe19139cac7514a1.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: Data Fiduciaries and, where applicable, Consent Managers must publish how Data Principals can exercise rights and the grievance-response period, and implement measures to support responses within that period. The notice provisions also require means for complaints to the Board.

Force: Historical text; not used to infer current duties. Implementation independently verified: no.

> the period under its grievance redressal system for responding to the grievances of Data Principals

Rule 13(3), Gazette p. 35. [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/02/f8a8e97a91091543fe19139cac7514a1.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The draft requires reasonable security safeguards, including encryption or masking, access controls, logs, backups, and a one-year retention period for specified logs and personal data. It also requires notice to affected Data Principals without delay and notice to the Board, including detailed information within 72 hours unless a longer period is allowed.

Force: Historical text; not used to infer current duties. Implementation independently verified: no.

> securing of such personal data through its encryption, obfuscation or masking or the use of virtual tokens mapped to that personal data

Rule 6(1)(a), Gazette p. 30. [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/02/f8a8e97a91091543fe19139cac7514a1.pdf).
