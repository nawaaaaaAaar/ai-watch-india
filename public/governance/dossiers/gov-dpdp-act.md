## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: Establishes the Data Protection Board of India, which can inquire into breaches, issue directions, and impose penalties. The Board is to function independently and, as far as practicable, as a digital office.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The Board shall function as an independent body and shall, as far as practicable, function as a digital office

Printed p. 14, section 28(1). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: Requires Significant Data Fiduciaries to appoint an independent data auditor and undertake periodic data protection impact assessments and audits. The Central Government may designate fiduciaries as significant based on listed factors, including data volume, sensitivity, and risks.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> periodic Data Protection Impact Assessment

Printed p. 9, section 10(2)(c)(i). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: Does not establish a general procurement or vendor-selection framework. A Data Fiduciary may engage a Data Processor for relevant activities only under a valid contract, and remains responsible for covered processing undertaken on its behalf.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> only under a valid contract

Printed p. 7, section 8(2). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: Requires Data Fiduciaries and Consent Managers to provide grievance redressal; a Data Principal must exhaust that opportunity before approaching the Board. The Board may inquire into qualifying complaints, and its orders or directions may be appealed to the Appellate Tribunal.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The Data Principal shall exhaust the opportunity of redressing her grievance under this section before approaching the Board.

Printed p. 10, section 13(3). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Requires reasonable security safeguards and breach intimation to the Board and affected Data Principals. It also requires erasure when consent is withdrawn or the specified purpose is no longer served, unless retention is legally necessary.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> A Data Fiduciary shall protect personal data in its possession or under its control

Printed p. 7, section 8(5). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf).
