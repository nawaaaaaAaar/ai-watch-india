## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: For specified notices, an officer must be authorised by written order. Where police administration issues an intimation, each authorised officer must be at least Deputy Inspector General of Police and specially authorised by the Appropriate Government; no independent oversight body is established here.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> issued by an officer authorised, by order in writing, for the purpose of issuing such intimation

Printed p. 11, rule 3(1)(d)(ii)(I). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/f55fe52418b03f58b0669f6a8bc03b6d.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: A significant social media intermediary must, before displaying, uploading, or publishing information, require a user declaration on whether it is synthetically generated and use appropriate technical measures to verify the declaration, considering the information’s nature, format, and source.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> deploy appropriate technical measures, including automated tools or other suitable mechanisms, to verify the accuracy of such declaration, having regard to the nature, format, and source of such information

Printed p. 13, rule 4(1A)(b). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/f55fe52418b03f58b0669f6a8bc03b6d.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: A violation may lead to disclosure, in accordance with applicable law, of the violating user’s identity to a complainant who is a victim or acts on a victim’s behalf. The text also provides for expeditious and appropriate intermediary action when specified violations come to its attention, including through a complaint.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> disclosure of the identity of the violating user to the complainant, where such complainant is a victim of, or an individual acting on behalf of a victim of, such contravention

Printed p. 10, rule 3(1)(ca)(ii)(III). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/f55fe52418b03f58b0669f6a8bc03b6d.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Intermediaries must prominently label covered synthetic information and, to the extent technically feasible, embed permanent metadata or another provenance mechanism, including a unique identifier that can identify the intermediary’s computer resource used to create or alter it. They must not enable modification, suppression, or removal of the label or metadata.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> such information shall be embedded with a permanent metadata or other appropriate technical provenance mechanisms, to the extent technically feasible, including a unique identifier

Printed p. 12, rule 3(3)(a)(ii). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/f55fe52418b03f58b0669f6a8bc03b6d.pdf).
