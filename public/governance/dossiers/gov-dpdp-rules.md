## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: The rules provide for Board appointments, meetings, inquiries, and digital proceedings. Board inquiries ordinarily must be completed within six months, with written reasons required for extensions of up to three months at a time.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The inquiry by the Board shall be completed within a period of six months from the date of receipt

Printed p. 31, Rule 19(9). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: Significant Data Fiduciaries must conduct a data protection impact assessment and audit every twelve months and submit a report of significant observations to the Board. They must also exercise due diligence regarding whether adopted technical measures, including algorithmic software, may risk Data Principals’ rights.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> A Significant Data Fiduciary shall, once in every period of twelve months

Printed p. 29, Rule 13(1). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).

### Procurement

Not established in reviewed text: A general procurement or mandatory vendor-selection framework is not located in the reviewed text; this is not proof of absence. The rules do require appropriate security provisions in applicable contracts between a Data Fiduciary and a Data Processor, but do not establish a general procurement duty.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

Limit: No concrete provision established in this reviewed text; not proof of absence elsewhere

### Redress

Textual provision: Data Fiduciaries and Consent Managers must publish their grievance-redress period, capped at ninety days, and implement measures to make the system effective. Notices must also identify means for Data Principals to complain to the Board.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> within a reasonable period not exceeding ninety days under its grievance redressal system

Printed p. 30, Rule 14(3). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Data Fiduciaries must apply reasonable safeguards to personal data, including access controls, logging and monitoring, breach investigation and remediation, backups, and relevant processor-contract provisions. The rule also requires retaining specified logs and personal data for one year, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Force: Enacted text; scope, amendments and commencement require checking. Implementation independently verified: no.

> by taking reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breach

Printed p. 26, Rule 6(1). [Read the original](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).
