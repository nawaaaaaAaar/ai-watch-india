## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: The recommendations call for regular audits and assessments of the AIBOM process. The reviewed text does not establish an AI-specific oversight authority or describe its powers.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Regular audits and assessments of the AIBOM process should be conducted to ensure its accuracy, completeness, and compliance with organizational and regulatory standards.

Printed p. 57, §9.4.1.10. [Read the original](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/TechnicalGuidelines-on-SBOM,QBOM&CBOM,AIBOM_and_HBOM_ver2.0.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: The minimum AIBOM elements include model performance metrics, with examples such as accuracy, precision, recall and F1 score. The text does not specify evaluation thresholds or a required evaluation protocol.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Metrics for evaluating the performance of the AI model, such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score, etc.

Printed p. 54, §9.3, Table 10. [Read the original](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/TechnicalGuidelines-on-SBOM,QBOM&CBOM,AIBOM_and_HBOM_ver2.0.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: Section 9.4.1 recommends including AIBOM requirements in AI-related procurements and solutions. It also says supplied AI solutions must be accompanied by an AIBOM; these appear within a recommendations section, not an identified statutory direction.

Force: Recommendation or guidance; not a new enacted obligation. Implementation independently verified: no.

> All government, public sector, essential services organizations, and organizations involved in AI-driven development and services should include AIBOM requirements in all AI-related procurements and solutions.

Printed p. 56, §9.4.1.1. [Read the original](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/TechnicalGuidelines-on-SBOM,QBOM&CBOM,AIBOM_and_HBOM_ver2.0.pdf).
