## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: The policy establishes a two-layer Odisha AI Mission structure: an AI Taskforce for strategic direction and an AI Cell under OCAC for implementation. The Taskforce is chaired by the Principal Secretary, E&IT Department; the text also calls for a dedicated monitoring body for ethical AI, without specifying its composition.

Force: Administrative or policy commitment; not proof of activity or outcomes. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The mission will have a two-layered structure, comprising an AI task force and an AI cell.

Printed p. 17, §6 Convergence. [Read the original](https://it.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-01/4602-E&IT%20Deptt.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: The Taskforce is to track AI Cell initiatives through KPIs, and the phased approach calls for a monitoring and evaluation framework, progress tracking, and reporting. The policy also proposes a centralized performance dashboard for outcome measurement.

Force: Administrative or policy commitment; not proof of activity or outcomes. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Track progress of the initiatives being carried out by the AI Cell, through Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

Printed p. 17, §6.1 Mandate of the AI Taskforce. [Read the original](https://it.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-01/4602-E&IT%20Deptt.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: The phased implementation plan includes developing solution designs and issuing RFPs for vendor onboarding. The reviewed text does not establish vendor-selection criteria or a general mandatory procurement framework; the RFP provision is a planned implementation activity, not a rule selecting vendors.

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: The policy calls for data-governance standards and procedures, alignment with national data frameworks and the DPDP Act, and mandatory certification for developers and companies. It also assigns the AI Cell regular reviews of AI models for bias and privacy-law compliance and calls for traceability and transparency of AI-generated outputs.

Force: Administrative or policy commitment; not proof of activity or outcomes. Implementation independently verified: no.

> through mandatory certification for developers and companies.
