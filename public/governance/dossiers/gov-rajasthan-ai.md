## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: DoIT&C is the administrative department. The policy provides for approval and evaluation committees and departmental AI Nodal Officers to coordinate and oversee initiatives.

Force: Policy text; operative notification and enforcement not independently established. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The administrative department governing the policy will be the Department of Information Technology & Communication (DoIT&C), Government of Rajasthan.

### Evaluation

Textual provision: Government AI deployments are to receive periodic performance reviews, bias assessments, and risk audits. The policy also calls for a risk-classification framework and pre-deployment impact assessments for critical AI systems.

Force: Policy text; operative notification and enforcement not independently established. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Periodic performance reviews, bias assessments, and risk detection audits will ensure reliability and public confidence.

Printed p. 22, section 7.1; image checked. [Read the original](https://ai.rajasthan.gov.in/policy/AI%20ML%20POLICY.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: Government AI procurement is to follow CERT-In AIBOM guidance, covering system components such as hardware, datasets, software frameworks, and models. This provision does not establish mandatory vendor selection criteria.

Force: Policy text; operative notification and enforcement not independently established. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Procurement shall follow CERT-In’s Artificial Intelligence Bill of Material’ (AIBOM) guidelines

Printed p. 22, section 7.1; image checked. [Read the original](https://ai.rajasthan.gov.in/policy/AI%20ML%20POLICY.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: For governance deployments, the State identifies Rajasthan Sampark and reporting dashboards for monitoring, with escalation procedures for harmful, unintended, or discriminatory outcomes. A separate appeal is available against Project Approval Committee orders, to be filed within 30 days.

Force: Policy text; operative notification and enforcement not independently established. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The State will leverage the existing grievance redressal platform- Rajasthan Sampark and reporting dashboards for monitoring Al

Printed pp. 22–23, section 7.1; not the incentive-order appeal. [Read the original](https://ai.rajasthan.gov.in/policy/AI%20ML%20POLICY.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: The policy requires meaningful information about AI systems and an accountability structure for decisions. It also specifies informed consent, purpose limitation, data minimization, and anonymization or pseudonymization for public datasets.

Force: Policy text; operative notification and enforcement not independently established. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Meaningful and timely information about every Al system shall be provided to all relevant stakeholders, tailored to their knowledge and accessibility needs. An accountability structure shall govern decisions related to an Al system.

Printed p. 13, section 3.2(d); image checked. [Read the original](https://ai.rajasthan.gov.in/policy/AI%20ML%20POLICY.pdf).
