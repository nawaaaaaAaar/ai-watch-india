## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The draft proposes risk-tier classification of government AI systems and requires an AI Impact Assessment and Human Oversight Plan before high-risk systems are put into service. These are proposed policy provisions, not evidence of an adopted or operational oversight regime.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> High-Risk AI systems shall not be placed into service unless: a) An AI Impact Assessment (AIIA) has been completed and approved b) A Human Oversight Plan is in place

Section 4.4, p. 11. [Read the original](https://dit.goa.gov.in/assets/books/goa_ai_policy_draft.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The draft proposes annual third-party audits for high-risk systems, incident-triggered audits, and public reporting on AI adoption and incidents. The text does not verify that these evaluations have been carried out.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Annual third-party audits for high-risk systems; incident-triggered audits as required

Pillar VI, p. 18. [Read the original](https://dit.goa.gov.in/assets/books/goa_ai_policy_draft.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The draft proposes a Technical Assessment Memorandum for AI procurements exceeding ₹10 lakh and AI-related risk, assessment, documentation, and audit requirements in procurement clauses. These are draft proposals, not verified procurement practice.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Mandatory Technical Assessment Memorandum (TAM) for all AI procurements exceeding ₹10 lakh

Pillar III, p. 15. [Read the original](https://dit.goa.gov.in/assets/books/goa_ai_policy_draft.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The draft proposes an AI Complaints Channel and a dedicated Cyber Cell support desk for deepfake harms. For deepfake complaints, it sets proposed targets for acknowledgement, platform takedown requests, and victim updates; it does not establish that the channel or targets are operational.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> acknowledgement of complaint within 24 hours; takedown request to relevant platform within 48 hours; investigation update to victim within 7 days.

Section 10.8, p. 35. [Read the original](https://dit.goa.gov.in/assets/books/goa_ai_policy_draft.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The draft assigns data-governance responsibilities to a State Chief Data Officer and proposes a public dataset catalogue with standardised schemas, metadata, and update frequencies. It also proposes annual status reporting and departmental AI-readiness assessments; implementation is not verified.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> The State Chief Data Officer shall maintain a publicly accessible dataset catalogue, with mandatory standardisation of schema, metadata, and update frequency across all contributing departments.

Pillar IV, p. 16. [Read the original](https://dit.goa.gov.in/assets/books/goa_ai_policy_draft.pdf).
