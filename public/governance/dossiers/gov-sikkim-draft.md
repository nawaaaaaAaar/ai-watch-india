## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The policy proposes an AI Centre of Excellence with powers to evaluate use cases, audit systems, conduct risk assessments, and stop or suspend high-risk projects. It also calls for an AI Ethics Committee to develop advisory guidance.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Stop or suspend high-risk AI projects

Printed p. 18, Core Principles—AI COE governance powers. [Read the original](https://www.sikkim.gov.in/uploads/DeptUploads/Draft_AI_Policy_2025_20260313.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The policy calls for bias assessments, testing, validation, and monitoring, and gives the AI COE a role in conducting risk assessments and auditing systems. It does not specify a single evaluation procedure or schedule.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Regular assessments for bias should be conducted.

Printed p. 14, Core Principles—Fairness & Non-discrimination. [Read the original](https://www.sikkim.gov.in/uploads/DeptUploads/Draft_AI_Policy_2025_20260313.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The government says it will adopt recommended AI procurement guidelines in consultation with the Ethics Working Committee. Explainability and interpretability are offered as possible considerations, not mandatory vendor-selection requirements.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> the government will adopt recommended AI procurement guidelines.

Printed p. 23, Key Pillars—Governance, Ethics and Privacy. [Read the original](https://www.sikkim.gov.in/uploads/DeptUploads/Draft_AI_Policy_2025_20260313.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The core principles call for mechanisms for redress, but the reviewed text does not establish a specific complaint process, forum, or remedy.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Mechanisms for audit, oversight, and redress.

Printed p. 14, Core Principles—Accountability. [Read the original](https://www.sikkim.gov.in/uploads/DeptUploads/Draft_AI_Policy_2025_20260313.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The policy calls for assigning responsibility for each AI system and defining data ownership, source, and use in training or validation. It also calls for lawful collection, secure storage, limited retention, and role-based access.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> There should be a clear definition of data ownership, data source, and how data is used in training / validation.

Printed p. 27, Data Handling and Privacy. [Read the original](https://www.sikkim.gov.in/uploads/DeptUploads/Draft_AI_Policy_2025_20260313.pdf).
