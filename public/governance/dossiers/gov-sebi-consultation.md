## Five complete coding dimensions

### Oversight

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The proposal describes an internal team with relevant skills to monitor models through their lifecycle and calls for designated senior management oversight. It also proposes risk controls, exception handling, fallback plans, and independent audits.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> There should be a designated senior management, having appropriate technical knowledge and experience, responsible for the oversight of the model development, validation, ongoing testing, deployment, monitoring and controls of AI/ML based models.

Printed p. 8, §5.1(d). [Read the original](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2025/1750415065695.pdf).

### Evaluation

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The proposal calls for continuous testing and monitoring, pre-deployment testing in an environment segregated from live operations, and shadow testing with live traffic before production deployment. It also proposes retaining model documentation and input and output data for at least five years.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> market participants should perform shadow testing with live traffic of AI/ML models to ensure quality and performance before deployment in production environment.

Printed p. 10, §5.3(c). [Read the original](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2025/1750415065695.pdf).

### Procurement

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The proposal addresses oversight of third-party AI/ML vendors, including monitoring their performance and using contracts or service-level agreements that clarify outsourced functions and remedies. It states that vendor-provided AI/ML services are deemed provided by market participants, but this is proposed text in a consultation paper, not an established duty here.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> However, AI and ML services provided by third-party vendors are deemed to be provided by the market participants, who shall be responsible for ensuring compliance with all applicable laws, rules and regulations.

Printed p. 9, §5.1(e). [Read the original](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2025/1750415065695.pdf).

### Redress

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The proposal says the investor grievance mechanism for AI/ML systems is to be in line with SEBI’s existing regulatory framework. The reviewed text does not specify a separate AI/ML grievance route.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Investor grievance mechanism for AI/ML systems shall be in line with existing regulatory framework of SEBI

Printed p. 10, §5.2(d). [Read the original](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2025/1750415065695.pdf).

### Accountability and data

Textual provision: Draft / consultation proposal: The proposal addresses model documentation, auditability, logs, periodic accuracy reporting to SEBI, and data governance, including ownership, access controls, encryption, and recording requests to unmask data. It also proposes data privacy and cybersecurity policies and compliance with applicable laws.

Force: Draft proposal; no obligation inferred. Implementation independently verified: no.

> Market participants should clearly define data governance norms which inter-alia shall include data ownership, access controls, encryption

Printed pp. 8–9, §5.1(g). [Read the original](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2025/1750415065695.pdf).
