## Rule 2(1A): Unlawful-information references retain SGI coverage

Collection: sgi. Comparison: Draft to final. Type: Continuity. Actor/class: Intermediaries. Earlier context: Draft amendment 2(ii).

Status: Notified amendment; operative-date and court-status limits apply.

### Textual observation

The draft's rule making unlawful-information references include SGI is carried into the final. [Draft amendment 2(ii)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/9de47fb06522b9e40a61e4731bc7de51.pdf) [Final amendment 2(b), Rule 2(1A); Gazette p. 9](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/f55fe52418b03f58b0669f6a8bc03b6d.pdf)

Earlier excerpt (CONSULTATION DRAFT, as extracted):

> For the purposes of these rules, any reference to ‘information’ in the context of information being used to commit an unlawful act, including under clause (b) and (d) of sub-rule (1) of rule 3 and sub-rules (2) and (4) of rule 4, shall be construed to include synthetically generated information, unless the context otherwise requires

Later excerpt (NOTIFIED AMENDMENT, as printed, as extracted):

> For the purposes of these rules, any reference to ‘information’ in the context of information being used to commit an unlawful act, including under clauses (b) and (d) of sub-rule (1) of rule 3 and sub-rules (2) and (4) of rule 4, shall be construed to include synthetically generated information, unless the context otherwise requires.

### Analyst interpretation

This is continuity, not a newly invented standalone offence for every use of synthetic media.

### Limits and follow-up

The qualifying context and underlying applicable law still matter.

Reporting question: Are public explanations distinguishing underlying illegality from the mere use of AI?
