## Rule 12: Children's-data exemptions

Collection: dpdp. Comparison: Draft to final. Type: Continuity. Actor/class: Children and parents. Earlier context: Rule 11.

### Textual observation

Exemption mechanism retained, while Fourth Schedule changes materially. Renumbered body; linked schedule change. [Draft, p. 34](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/02/f8a8e97a91091543fe19139cac7514a1.pdf#page=34) [Final, p. 29](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf#page=29)

Earlier excerpt (consultation draft, as extracted):

> 11. Exemptions from certain obligations applicable to processing of personal data of child.—(1) The provisions of sub-sections (1) and (3) of section 9 of the Act shall not be applicable to processing of personal data of a child by such class of Data Fiduciaries as are specified in Part A of Fourth Schedule, subject to such conditions as are specified in the said Part. (2) The provisions of sub-sections (1) and (3) of section 9 of the Act shall not be applicable to processing of personal data of a child for such purposes as are specified in Part B of Fourth Schedule, subject to such conditions as are specified in the said Part.

Later excerpt (final, as printed, as extracted):

> 12. Exemptions from certain obligations applicable to processing of personal data of child. — (1) The provisions of sub-sections (1) and (3) of section 9 of the Act shall not be applicable to processing of personal data of a child by such class of Data Fiduciaries as are specified in Part A of Fourth Schedule, subject to such conditions as are specified in the said Part. (2) The provisions of sub-sections (1) and (3) of section 9 of the Act shall not be applicable to processing of personal data of a child for such purposes as are specified in Part B of Fourth Schedule, subject to such conditions as are specified in the said Part.
