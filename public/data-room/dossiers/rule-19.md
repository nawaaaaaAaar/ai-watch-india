Earlier excerpt (consultation draft, as extracted):

> 18. Procedure for meetings of Board and authentication of its orders, directions and instruments.—(1) The Chairperson shall fix the date, time and place of meetings of the Board, approve the items of agenda therefor, and cause notice specifying the same to be issued under her signature or that of such other individual as the Chairperson may authorise by general or special order in writing. (2) Meetings of the Board shall be chaired by the Chairperson and, in her absence, by such other Member as the Members present at the meeting may choose from amongst themselves. (3) One-third of the membership of the Board shall be the quorum for its meetings.
