## Part 2.1: Infrastructure becomes an explicit governance pillar

Collection: aig. Comparison: Recommendation lineage. Type: Scope change. Actor/class: AI developers, regulators and public institutions. Earlier context: Consultation report introduction; printed p. 1.

Status: Recommendations; not a new standalone legal mandate.

### Textual observation

Infrastructure moves from mission background to a dedicated pillar covering data, compute and DPI. [Consultation report introduction; printed p. 1](https://indiaai.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/docs/subcommittee-report-dec26.pdf) [Part 2.1; printed pp. 14-16](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc2025115685601.pdf)

Earlier excerpt (CONSULTATION REPORT, as extracted):

> The IndiaAI mission will establish a comprehensive ecosystem catalysing AI innovation through strategic programs and partnerships across the public and private sectors. By democratizing computing access, improving data quality, developing indigenous AI capabilities, attracting top AI talent, enabling industry collaboration, providing startup risk capital, ensuring socially impactful AI projects and bolstering ethical AI, it will drive responsible, inclusive growth of India's AI ecosystem.

Later excerpt (PUBLISHED GUIDELINES, as printed, as extracted):

> Empower the India AI mission, line ministries, sectoral regulators and state governments to increase AI adoption through initiatives on infrastructure development and increasing access to data and computing resources.

### Analyst interpretation

Governance is framed as enabling access and safety evaluation as well as setting restrictions.

### Limits and follow-up

Recommendation lineage, not a one-to-one legal deletion/insertion. The historical infrastructure figures in the November report are not verified current inventory or scheme eligibility.

Reporting question: Who receives affordable compute and representative evaluation datasets, and how is access assessed?
