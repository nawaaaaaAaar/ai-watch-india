## Amendment 1: An express commencement date

Collection: sgi. Comparison: Draft to final. Type: Timing. Actor/class: Intermediaries. Earlier context: Draft amendment 1.

Status: Notified amendment; operative-date and court-status limits apply.

### Textual observation

The draft's blank date becomes 20 February 2026 in the notification. [Draft amendment 1](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/9de47fb06522b9e40a61e4731bc7de51.pdf) [Final amendment 1; Gazette p. 8](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/f55fe52418b03f58b0669f6a8bc03b6d.pdf)

Earlier excerpt (CONSULTATION DRAFT, as extracted):

> (2) They shall come into force on the ___ day of________, 2025.

Later excerpt (NOTIFIED AMENDMENT, as printed, as extracted):

> (2) They shall come into force on the 20 th day of February, 2026.

### Analyst interpretation

A specified operative date replaces the placeholder. It is not the same as the notification's 10 February date.
