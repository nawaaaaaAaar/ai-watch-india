## SUVAS judicial translation: SUVAS judicial translation: decision-accountability case

Collection: service. Comparison: System claim to accountability evidence. Type: Public-service case. Actor/class: Judicial access. Earlier context: Judgment translation section.

Status: Official use reported.

### Textual observation

The official backgrounder describes translations hosted on e-SCR and judicial translation committees; a separate Allahabad notice documents a local vetting workflow. [Judgment translation section](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2226283&reg=48&lang=2) [Translation committee oversight](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2226283&reg=48&lang=2)

Earlier excerpt (SYSTEM RECORD, as extracted):

> For this, **SUVAS** **(Supreme Court Vidhik Anuvaad Software)**, an AI-driven translation tool of the Supreme Court, converts English judgments and orders into vernacular languages, enhancing accessibility and regional language use in courts. These translated judgments are hosted on the **e-SCR portal**, significantly expanding public access.

Later excerpt (SUPPORTING RECORD, as printed, as extracted):

> **Supreme Court and High Court AI Translation Committees** oversee quality and constitutional accuracy. AI use remains assistive, with translations reviewed within judicial frameworks to support access to justice.

### Analyst interpretation

A translation aids access to a judgment but is not an automated judicial determination. The local vetting record shows concrete human review, without establishing one nationwide SOP or a current universal correction route.

### Limits and follow-up

The 2023 Allahabad process is jurisdiction-specific and historical. Do not infer that its remuneration, panel or disclaimer governs every court or every current SUVAS output.

Reporting question: Which decision can this system influence, what evidence supports its use, and how can a person obtain human review?

### Complete accountability matrix

#### Owner & purpose

Evidence status: Documented.

Supreme Court SUVAS translates judgments and orders to vernacular languages.

[Official judicial AI backgrounder: Judgment translation section](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2226283&reg=48&lang=2)

> For this, **SUVAS** **(Supreme Court Vidhik Anuvaad Software)**, an AI-driven translation tool of the Supreme Court, converts English judgments and orders into vernacular languages, enhancing accessibility and regional language use in courts. These translated judgments are hosted on the **e-SCR portal**, significantly expanding public access.

#### Decision role & affected people

Evidence status: Documented.

Translation supports people reading court records; it is not itself a decision on the merits of a case.
