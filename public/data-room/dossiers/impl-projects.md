## Responsible AI portfolio: Thirteen approved projects are not thirteen validated tools

Collection: impl. Comparison: Recommendation to implementation evidence. Type: Evidence checkpoint. Actor/class: IndiaAI, MeitY and oversight bodies. Earlier context: Part 2.6; printed p. 36.

Status: Programme activity reported.

### Textual observation

The July official record reports 13 approved Responsible AI projects across safety themes. [Part 2.6; printed p. 36](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc2025115685601.pdf) [27 July release, Safe & Trusted AI pillar](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2289946&reg=3&lang=1)

Earlier excerpt (POLICY RECOMMENDATION, as extracted):

> Further, the ongoing work under the IndiaAI mission to support the development of technical solutions to address issues relating to machine unlearning, bias mitigation, privacy-enhancing tools, explainable AI, etc. should also continue.

Later excerpt (REVIEWED IMPLEMENTATION RECORD, as printed, as extracted):

> 13 Responsible AI projects have been approved in educational institutions across the country. These include topics such as bias mitigation, machine unlearning, privacy-preserving AI, explainability, deepfake detection and AI risk assessment.

### Analyst interpretation

There is documentary evidence of a selected programme portfolio, but approvals and ongoing development are not completion certificates, independent benchmarks or proof that every tool is available.

### Limits and follow-up

The mission-wide financial outlay is not an institute-specific or project-specific expenditure figure. No portfolio-wide effectiveness score is assigned.

Reporting question: For each approved project, what was funded, delivered, evaluated and released?
