# AI Watch: institutional coverage and source recheck

Dataset v1.1.0, documentary cutoff 5 October 2026. This research round fixes five ministry documentary frames and the independent Supreme Court, adds eighteen source-coded systems/services, and attempts to re-locate every original source excerpt. It expands usable data without claiming a national census, independent second coding or proof of actual operational effectiveness.

## The frame and what it covers

Four comparable searches for each entry cover deployment descriptions, procurement, evaluation/safeguards and October recency. Twelve named-candidate follow-ups supplement the 24 frame queries. All 36 queries and 228 returned discovery hits are logged; hits are leads, not evidence. Original sources were fetched and the selected text checked before coding.

| Documentary frame | Associated system records | New additions | Scope limit |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Agriculture and Farmers Welfare | 4 | NPSS; CROPIC | Named departmental programmes, not every agricultural AI project or collaborator |
| Health and Family Welfare | 6 | MadhuNetrAI; CATB; Khushi; Media Disease Surveillance; BODH | BODH is evaluation infrastructure; an AFMS collaboration does not establish a Defence census |
| Education | 2 | e-Jaadui Pitara bots; DIKSHA accessibility | AI teaching and courses are not automatically AI deployments |
| Home Affairs | 4 | CCTNS 2.0 components; Abhigyan; CrPI | Biometric cases are qualified; planned predictive components are not presumed live |
| Railways | 6 | MVIS; ITMS; VSS analytics; RailMadad prioritisation | No claim that every installation has every model/module |
| Supreme Court | 4 | SuSahayak; TERES | Independent court institution, not ministry ownership or all-court coverage |

Twenty-six records are associated with the frame; ten earlier cases remain outside it as context. These counts describe inclusion in this documentary round, not institutional prevalence. The live dataset has 36 system/service records and retains all twelve accountability dimensions for every one.

## Findings and coding decisions

### Agriculture: a real dated update, and a carefully bounded assessment gap

The July parliamentary explanation distinguishes Bharat-VISTAAR telephone availability in Hindi and English from chatbot/web/app availability in ten languages. This refines the earlier March phase description without resolving the undated portal's 22+ language claim or making all channels interchangeable. The new observation and earlier uncertainty both remain in the data. ([July answer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2289031&reg=3&lang=1), [Service portal](https://vistaar.da.gov.in/))

The same answer reports NPSS use by more than 10,000 extension workers, supporting 73 crops and 436 pests. The earlier NPSS explanation states launch on 15 August 2024 and describes image-based AI/ML identification and advisory. Coverage and advisory volume do not supply diagnostic error rates. ([July answer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2289031&reg=3&lang=1), [NPSS explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2114896))

The government explicitly says no specific comprehensive assessment of AI-based technology impact had been undertaken. That is a meaningful disclosed limitation, but not proof that NPSS has never had a component test, security assessment or evaluation outside that question's scope. Broad digital-agriculture budget figures in the reply are not assigned as NPSS or CROPIC spending. ([Parliamentary explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2289031&reg=3&lang=1))

CROPIC's government-developer listing describes geotagged crop photographs and uploaded location/date/crop metadata analysed by AI, yet its data-safety section declares “No data collected.” The dataset flags this declaration tension rather than assert illegal collection: app-store definitions, processor roles and actual flows require further investigation. A support email is not evidence of a tested appeal against an insurance-related AI error. ([CROPIC listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.application.pmfby.cropic&hl=en_US))

### Health: separate screening, patient support, surveillance and validation infrastructure

The ministry's December 2025 explanation reports MadhuNetrAI across 38 facilities in eleven states and more than 14,000 retinal images. A separate AFMS launch describes seven pilot locations to be implemented and specialist referral for vision-threatening retinopathy. These are distinct observations; the planned seven-site implementation is not coded as seven completed operational sites or an independent accuracy benchmark. ([Health parliamentary explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2199422&reg=3&lang=1), [AFMS launch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2204763&reg=3&lang=1))

CATB's reported additional TB yield of 12–16% is stored as a numeric range with its claim type and an unresolved comparator/protocol. It is not converted into sensitivity, specificity or causal proof of nationwide effectiveness. The ministry's screening-volume claim remains usage evidence. ([Health explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2199422&reg=3&lang=1))

The July reply names Khushi chatbot support in the TB Mukt Bharat app and Media Disease Surveillance under IDSP as part of the operational AI portfolio. These enter separate system records because treatment support and outbreak monitoring are different functions. The ministry's general safeguards language is not treated as proof of each product's retention enforcement, override SOP or successful remedy. ([Public-healthcare answer](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS186_TDs0LP.pdf?source=lsapps))

BODH is officially described as a National Health Authority/IIT Kanpur platform for testing performance, robustness, bias and generalisability against anonymised health data. It is coded as AI evaluation infrastructure, not itself a clinical diagnostic deployment or proof that all listed health AI has passed evaluation. SAHI stays a strategy/context record, not another deployed model. ([SAHI/BODH launch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2229226&reg=3&lang=1))

### Education: using AI is not the same as teaching AI

The NCERT portal advertises e-Jaadui Pitara AI bots on WhatsApp and Telegram for parents and teachers. DIKSHA's government description separately identifies AI video keyword search and read-aloud accessibility. Each gets its own bounded service record; neither is reimagined as automated admissions or student-risk scoring. No chatbot interaction or child personal data was submitted. ([NCERT portal](https://ejaaduipitara.ncert.gov.in/), [Education backgrounder](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2234853&reg=3&lang=1))

AI courses, curricula and training programmes are not automatically deployment evidence. The same backgrounder's Perplexity–AICTE onboarding is retained as an access/training partnership rather than inflated into an AI decision system. A related National Learning Operating System EOI is issued by DIC under MeitY, outside the Education administrative frame; an EOI/cum-market assessment is not an award or operational rollout. ([Education backgrounder](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2234853&reg=3&lang=1), [DIC expression of interest](https://dic.gov.in/add_tender/expression-of-interest-eoi-for-ai-enabled-edtech-platform-for-national-learning-operating-system/))

### Home Affairs: preserve the version problem, and do not call every application AI

April reporting of a parliamentary submission describes CCTNS 2.0 as under development with contemplated AI tools. A June government backgrounder says a revamped AI-powered CCTNS 2.0 launched in 2024. Different components or versions may explain the difference, but that relationship is not established here, so the dataset preserves the conflict instead of certifying live predictive-policing components. ([Parliamentary-submission reporting](https://government.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/technology/cctns-20-indias-police-network-to-use-ai-for-crime-prediction-and-criminal-profiling/129942598), [Government backgrounder](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2275007))

The June launch describes Abhigyan fingerprint matching and CrPI multimodal facial/iris/DNA identification. Both are separately qualified biometric contexts, without claiming a verified model-specific AI architecture. Matching a suspect's biometric record is not guilt or conviction. The other launched applications, e-Prosecution and e-Forensics, are not automatically called AI because they appeared in the same announcement. ([NCRB application launch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2275306&reg=3&lang=1))

### Railways: distinguish installations, modules, interview reports and requirements

The March parliamentary explanation reports six MVIS freight-stock pilot installations and three ITMS deployments. The records preserve alerting and maintenance-assistance functions without fabricating false-alarm rates, accepted contracts or measured accident reductions. Other technologies in the same release, such as WILD and OMRS, are not presumed model-specific AI merely from the umbrella headline. ([Railway explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2238772&reg=3&lang=2))

The April VSS statement reports surveillance coverage across 1,874 stations and describes AI intrusion/loitering analytics and facial recognition. The station count is not a verified count of each AI module at every station. Current match-review, retention and adverse-action procedures remain unverified in this corpus. ([Railway surveillance statement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2249823&reg=3&lang=1))

RailMadad's complaint classification/priority and BHASHINI voice integration are attributed to a CRIS managing-director interview. This is relevant grievance-decision evidence, but it does not establish fair queue prioritisation or an appeal against classification. Other named railway apps and development projects stay in an adjacent/deferred register where product/version records were insufficiently resolved in this round. ([CRIS interview](https://indianexpress.com/article/business/indian-railways-ai-integration-railmadad-railone-coach-mitra-10543224/))

### Courts: information access and transcription are not adjudication

The court site identifies SuSahayak as a VANI-powered AI assistant developed by NIC and warns that it supplies general information, with original records authoritative. The interface observation is not a launch date, legal advice, current accuracy score or a tested legal remedy. ([Supreme Court service site](https://www.sci.gov.in/))

The court white paper identifies TERES transcription use; the 2023 RFP requires qualified transcriber checking followed by reviewers. The RFP is not a verified current executed TERES contract, and its review requirement does not establish actual current compliance. A draft AI regulation or research white paper is not silently treated as enacted binding regulation. ([Court white paper](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec0490f1f4972d133619a60c30f3559e/uploads/2025/11/2025112244.pdf), [Transcription RFP](https://main.sci.gov.in/pdf/TN/23052023_062513.pdf))

## Source reliability is itself part of the dataset

Every one of the fifty used baseline source URLs was retried without cache, and all 125 baseline excerpts were tested against fresh cleaned extraction. Where that returned incomplete material, a raw-HTML fallback was tried. Twenty-seven sources re-located all their excerpts; twenty sources failed fresh retrieval; three returned only partial matching extraction. Sixty-eight original excerpts were re-located in those fresh attempts. These are technical retrieval outcomes, not independent truth judgments.

Unresolved originals were then retried through cached source extraction. Their original selections were re-located, but this is not fresh live verification; that basis is recorded separately. The cached retry cannot convert a current timeout, cookie-only page or parser omission into a claim of present availability.

One AskDISHA authorized-partner label was not re-located in fresh extraction. The data narrows its wording to the earlier captured interface and historical report; absence from the parser is not used as proof of affiliation termination. Another CAG launch-passage mismatch is treated as formatting/extraction uncertainty, not an institutional correction. Earlier snapshots, dates and source identity remain intact.

All 216 original accountability assertions receive a source-availability/text-match triage record. This does not constitute manual independent recoding, inter-rater agreement or a new operational audit of all 432 expanded fields. The twenty new used source selections were separately checked against fetched originals in this research pass.

## What is now usable

The release distributes 36 dossiers, 21 joinable CSV tables, full JSON, SQLite, codebook, protocol, SQL, both research rounds' logs, source-recheck outcomes and a checksummed package. Queries can distinguish the institutional cohort from earlier context, clinical use from evaluation infrastructure, fresh matches from cached extraction, and typed evidence from unknowns.

The contribution is better-denominated and better-qualified data, not a larger “AI transparency score.” It supports a pilot evidence-disclosure study and reporting leads, but cannot establish national prevalence, lack of all safeguards, causal impacts or researcher demand. Independent recoding and a genuine administrative/geographic inventory remain future work, not achievements attributed to this single-analyst round.
