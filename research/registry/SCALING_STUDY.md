# AI Watch: doubling the registry with accountable units

Dataset v1.3.0, documentary cutoff 5 October 2026. This release adds 72 named families to the preserved 72-record registry, giving 144 records and 1,728 accountability slots across 21 linked tables. Every field remains available, including unknowns; complete field retention is not complete disclosure or independent validation.

## What is and is not being doubled

The unit is a named institutional system/service family. Citizen-facing applications, institutional assistance, government enabling infrastructure, statutory professional-body services, proposed upgrades and historical public-sector/defence capabilities are differentiated by `system_kind`, stage, AI qualification and research cohort. This is not 144 verified live AI deployments, a national census or a prevalence estimate.

The original six-institution frame remains 26 associated records. Its denominator is not silently expanded when adding state services or public-sector institutions; the other 118 records are outside that earlier fixed frame. The four cohorts contain 18, 18, 36 and 72 records, and can be filtered or analysed separately.

The earlier BharatGen seed exclusion is retained as a historical scope decision. This broader round explicitly includes a named foundation-service family under enabling infrastructure, not a claim that model releases prove citizen-facing deployment. Domain-specific services and enabling frameworks are distinct layers, not interchangeable observations. Future research can exclude infrastructure, historical prototypes or qualified contexts rather than unknowingly pool them.

## Research process and reproducibility

This round records 591 discovery, identity, recency and accountability queries with 2511 returned leads and 0 query errors. The last named-candidate pass improved ambiguous acronym/country searches; irrelevant results were not coded. Search volume is not a measure of comprehensiveness or evidence strength.

There are 228 retrieval receipts, including 41 explicit failed receipts. An error is a retrieval event, not necessarily a unique inaccessible URL; recovered attempts remain in the log. Some nominally successful initial extracts contained cookie banners or incomplete text. Cached recovery and default cache-enabled extraction are explicitly described, not misrepresented as fresh live verification.

All 78 used new source records have selected quotations checked for normalised membership in fetched full text. Published selections are bounded to 250 words per original URL across newly used and earlier versions. Some source URLs recur across release-specific source records; counts are source records, not necessarily unique websites or URLs. Original copyrighted binaries and full scraped articles are not mirrored. Dates from document bodies take priority; publisher metadata is explicitly labelled when used, and undated pages stay undated.

`BASELINE_1_2_0.json` freezes every row value from all 21 prior tables before compilation. Tests compare recursive canonical hashes of all those rows, not merely a handful of examples. Snapshot hashes identify selected UTF-8 excerpts, not authenticated originals or proof of truth. Build reproducibility establishes package consistency, not independent recoding or agency-claim accuracy.

## Findings that change how the evidence should be used

- **MuleHunter processing boundaries**: bank-local processing and no bank-to-RBIH PII exchange are developer descriptions of one interface. Separate I4C collaboration reporting describes suspect-registry identifiers and training/model improvement; blanket “no personal-data sharing anywhere” would overstate the evidence. ([MuleHunter.ai technical documentation: RBIH](https://docs.rbihub.in/mule-hunter); [Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre, RBIH sign MoU to strengthen AI-based detection of mule accounts](https://government.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/secure-india/i4c-and-rbih-join-forces-to-combat-cyber-fraud-with-ai-technology/131059334))
- **Nutrition authentication metrics**: Poshan's 97.01% eKYC/facematching completion is an administrative completion measure, not face-recognition accuracy or proof that all authenticated people received rations. Temporary device-cache clearing does not establish server-side retention. ([Government of India - Press Release: Press Information Bureau](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2247561&reg=3&lang=1))
- **Judicial implementation**: the Andhra Pradesh order establishes a 1 October mandate with recorded-reason and technical fallback conditions. It does not independently establish that every courtroom is using the service, nor that vendor rollout counts represent acceptance or reliable operation. ([HIGH COURT OF ANDHRA PRADESH :: ...](https://aphc.gov.in/docs/notification_1785225296_0.pdf))
- **Pension audit controls**: CAG's EOI requires authorised officers to retain final sanction and specifies minimum five-year retention with legal hold/deletion capability. These are requirements and commissioning intent, not proof of awarded procurement or enforced production controls. ([tenders-EOI-for-Establishment-of-Sovereign-AI-Platform-for ...](https://cag.gov.in/uploads/tenders/tenders-EOI-for-Establishment-of-Sovereign-AI-Platform-for-CAG-of-India-069fb031304a1b8-89068885.pdf))
- **Teaching evaluation**: the Shiksha Copilot study abstract describes 1,043 teachers and 23 curators, with human customisation and systemic constraints. It is partner research reviewed at abstract level, not an independent causal evaluation of student achievement. ([Shiksha Copilot: Teacher-AI Collaboration for Curating and ...](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/teacher-ai-collaboration-for-curating-and-customizing-lesson-plans-in-low-resource-schools/))
- **Installed versus integrated traffic systems**: Nagpur reporting distinguishes more installed junction equipment from still-unfinished central integration; June counts use differing units. An October completion target is retained as a target, not converted into achieved coverage. ([Red or Green? Nagpur’s signals will now decide on their own](https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2026/6/17/red-or-green-nagpurs-signals-will-now-decide-on-their-own.html); [Smart signals multiply, but NMC’s traffic management system still awaits integration](https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2026/8/29/smart-signals-multiply-but-nmcs-traffic-management-system-still-awaits-integration.html))
- **Launch chronology**: Goa's government-service assistant had a February beta account before September announcements. Web and WhatsApp variants remain one family; the separate tourism-purpose service is not another channel of the same undifferentiated programme. ([Goa govt launches AI-powered digital assistant to simplify government](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/goa-govt-launches-aipowered-digital-assistant-to-simplify-government-services/143657.html); [Goa Government introduces AI Chatbot on WhatsApp to enhance citizen service delivery](https://www.expresscomputer.in/amp/news/goa-government-introduces-ai-chatbot-on-whatsapp-to-enhance-citizen-service-delivery/139115/); [Department of IT, Electronics and Communications (DITEC) launches Tourism AI Chatbot for Goa - Travel Media](https://www.travelmedia.in/2026/09/25/Department-of-IT-Electronics-Communications-DITE-launches-Tourism-AI-Chatbot-for-Goa.html))

Official input provenance does not alone establish faithful synthesis, correct joins, causal inference or completeness. ([Home](https://nfs.inroad.in/); [AI Saarthi Helps Policymakers Convert Government Data Into Actionable Insights: NITI Aayog](https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/economy/ai-saarthi-helps-policymakers-convert-government-data-into-actionable-insights-niti-aayog))
- **Funding boundaries**: BharatGen's ₹235 crore programme-support statement is not reconciled spending; the separately mentioned ₹10,585 crore IndiaAI Mission outlay is not added to BharatGen's budget. ([The Artificial Intelligence is no longer an option but an essential component of working in every sphere of life: says Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2229273&reg=48&lang=1))

Its zero-human-interface and 95% real-time-validation rhetoric do not establish absence of human exceptions or model accuracy. ## Reuse and limits

Use `research_round` and `system_kind` before forming any denominator. Join assertions through `evidence_links` to excerpts and sources; join typed metrics/evaluations rather than comparing numbers from prose. Do not pool usage, administrative completion, response-time observations, design requirements and diagnostic benchmarks into one performance metric.

The dataset supplies a research queue, not proof that undisclosed safeguards do not exist. Selection remains purposive and visibility-biased; there was no independent second coding, reliability estimate, field visit, user study, RTI response collection or personal-data submission. All earlier records and supporting policy collections remain separate and preserved. No public launch, paid services or external enquiries were undertaken.
