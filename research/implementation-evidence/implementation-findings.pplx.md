# What implementation records change about AI Watch

The important gain is not a higher system count, but a clearer chain between intentions, procurement stages, prescribed controls and reported operation. The ten-case layer makes these stages separately inspectable; none of the selected primary records supplies independent verification of effectiveness.

## Procurement exists before the headline, but is not the deployment

**NHAI Dashcam Analytics Services** has a procurement pathway before its March 2026 announcement: TCIL's July 2025 EOI includes the NHAI RFP, with weekly surveys, pre-financial-opening model evaluation, correction of reports within two working days and masking of faces and number plates. These requirements are stronger research anchors than a generic promise of AI-enabled monitoring, but they are not completed tests, a supplier award or implemented safeguards ([TCIL EOI and appended NHAI RFP](https://www.tcil.net.in/tender/pdf/25f2565.pdf)).

NHAI's March release describes planned coverage of around 40,000 km; June says rollout was initiated, not that it was complete. The unresolved next step is a contract-to-acceptance chain, not another announcement-based system row ([March announcement](https://nhai.gov.in/nhai/sites/default/files/2026-03/NHAI_Press_Release-NHAI-20-03-2026.pdf), [June status](https://nhai.gov.in/nhai/sites/default/files/2026-06/NHAI_Press_Release-16-06-2026-EN.pdf)).

**Railway IDS** has an RDSO pre-award POC requirement and a minimum six-month field-trial procedure that includes the rainy season ([RDSO specification](https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/2024-10-23-Signed%20specification%20EIDS.pdf)). Its 5% ceiling for physically verified false alarms is a specification, not an observed false-alarm rate; the ministerial account of 141 route-km implementation and tenders awarded for 981 route-km does not supply those completed trial results ([RDSO specification](https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/2024-10-23-Signed%20specification%20EIDS.pdf), [ministerial account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2199365&reg=3&lang=1)).

**Railway VSS** also needs component-level discipline: the Intellve invitation concerns integrating SPARSH NVRs with an ICCC application, not an established nationwide awarded supplier ([integration tender](https://www.railtel.in/download.php?f=MTc1MjU1MjI2OF9ET2MucGRm&fp=dXBsb2Fkcy90ZW5kZXJzL25vdGljZQ==)). RailTel's project page reports 2,077 commissioned stations without dating that count, so neither the invitation nor the page proves AI/FRS is active at every commissioned installation ([RailTel project page](https://www.railtel.in/key-projects/video-surveillance-system.html)).

## Formal oversight can be studied without claiming it works

**Adalat.AI** is backed by a named High Court direction, including reasoned manual exceptions for particular cases or technical difficulties, monthly reporting and mandatory onboarding. This creates concrete records to seek: actual use reports, exception counts and completed training, rather than treating the mandate itself as statewide implementation ([High Court circular](https://aphc.gov.in/docs/notification_1785225296_0.pdf)).

The subsequent training packet names Legsys Technology Foundation in the court communication and Legsys.AI Technologies Private Limited in the attachment; it does not establish that these are one legal entity. A scheduled September refresher programme is preparation evidence, not attendance evidence or an executed agreement ([district-court training packet](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec030b6ace9e8971cf36f1782aa982a7/uploads/2026/09/2026091091.pdf)).

**CAG PARAS and PARAKH** share one platform EOI that explicitly disclaims contractual obligation before an executed agreement, proposes POC weighting and requires human-in-the-loop controls and India-based institutional infrastructure. This is useful evidence of intended procurement architecture, but counting the two family links as separate procurements or treating broad audit-office directions as agent-specific deployment would overstate it ([CAG platform EOI](https://cag.gov.in/uploads/tenders/tenders-EOI-for-Establishment-of-Sovereign-AI-Platform-for-CAG-of-India-069fb031304a1b8-89068885.pdf)).

## Money and supplier roles require separate evidence

**Safe Kerala** has an original administrative sanction with monitoring-linked quarterly payments, alongside Keltron's account of the SRIT award and SLA ([Kerala administrative sanction](https://document.kerala.gov.in/documents/cabinetdecisions/cabinet2104202314:52:28.pdf), [Keltron implementation account](https://www.keltron.org/images/pdf/safekerala/SAFE_KERALA_PROJECT_Report_April_26.pdf)). The order supports authorised cost and payment conditions, not actual spending; the account is not the original third-party award or signed SLA, and the order's damaged Malayalam extraction requires the bounded visual-review warning retained in the data ([Kerala administrative sanction](https://document.kerala.gov.in/documents/cabinetdecisions/cabinet2104202314:52:28.pdf), [Keltron implementation account](https://www.keltron.org/images/pdf/safekerala/SAFE_KERALA_PROJECT_Report_April_26.pdf)).

**Madukkarai elephant surveillance** has an official sanction of Rs 724 lakh, a reported twelve-tower installation, a staffed control room and calls/SMS to locomotive pilots. These specify a human action chain and a funding stage, but do not establish expenditure, the supplier award, alert accuracy or a causal reduction in collisions ([Tamil Nadu launch release](https://cms.tn.gov.in/cms_migrated/document/press_release/pr090224_e_258.pdf)).

## Version continuity and conflicting chronology remain research questions

**UPSC** illustrates why old tender controls cannot simply be assigned to a later application. The 2024 biometric/facial-recognition/CCTV tender sets retention and SOP provisions; the 2026 official account attributes technical support for the own application to NeGD, but the retained records do not establish that this application is the same procurement or inherits those terms ([2024 UPSC tender](https://upsc.gov.in/sites/default/files/Tender-AadhaarQRFaceRecgAI-engl-250624.pdf), [2026 UPSC account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2268879&reg=3&lang=1)).

**IGMS** likewise separates legacy implementation from NextGen procurement: the latter specifies manual grievance closure, isolated LLM processing and a conditional engagement period ([NextGen RFP](https://www.darpg.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/031f6cdcc93bb39a667c7c824d5d757c.pdf)). Its clarification retains legacy data for training while separately requiring secure erasure of LLM-processing intermediates; these refer to different layers rather than automatically contradicting each other ([pre-bid clarification](https://www.darpg.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/7a9cc2262c76c3b3d02c5215fb38bf73.pdf)).

The chronology itself remains unresolved: the September 2023 monthly report dates IGMS 2.0's launch to 29 September, while a report labelled July 2025 places its launch among 2022 initiatives ([September 2023 report](https://www.darpg.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/8e377b8bc87d7f945fe94acd7159f7ab.pdf), [official July 2025 study](https://www.darpg.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/06/1976607cd8f0f723650e29d56afc38ff.pdf)). The data retains both official accounts rather than selecting a convenient date, and general platform appeals/privacy text is not promoted into proof of an AI-specific remedy or implemented model safeguard ([August 2026 account](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/aug/doc202689948801.pdf), [portal privacy policy](https://pgportal.gov.in/Home/Privacy)).

## Research use and remaining evidence gaps

This layer supports reproducible questions about documentary stage, prescribed oversight, procurement continuity and what remains unverified. It does not support prevalence estimates, compliance scores or effectiveness rankings: the ten cases were purposively selected, some sources are cached or undated, extraction is targeted for large documents, and coding remains single-analyst.

The highest-value next records are original awards and agreements, payment and acceptance records, actual exception/reviewer logs, dated activation inventories, completed evaluation reports and AI-specific appeals. Formal requirements now identify what to ask for more precisely; they do not establish that the institution followed them.
