# What the AI Watch recoding pass is designed to test

The methodological gain in this cycle is a challengeable procedure, not a completed reliability finding. A second researcher can independently reconstruct selected documentary evidence, challenge its classification and nominate omissions before seeing the first analyst's codes; the system count and existing research layers remain unchanged.

## Blinding is bounded, not absolute

The reviewer ZIP withholds original paraphrases, classifications, case conclusions, summaries, keyed identifiers and comparison values. The coordinator's separate private ZIP retains the linkage key and first-analyst reference; it must not be forwarded before the second pass is frozen.

System names and official source identities remain visible, and the originating dataset is already public. The protocol therefore provides conclusion-blinding by distribution and disclosure, not technical secrecy: prior familiarity, browsing the original analyses, deterministic-key reconstruction or discussions of expected results can defeat blinding. Those exposures must be declared, not erased by calling an audit “independent.”

The 124 observation fragments and ten-case frame were selected by the first analyst. Even a perfectly unexposed coder is testing this selection, not an independently sampled national corpus. Short excerpts can hide definitions, exceptions, dates or supporting clauses; original URLs, locators and bounded context are included, and inaccessible or unreadable originals can remain unresolved. Nominations explicitly allow omitted evidence and defective units to be challenged.

## Most vulnerable variables

- **Coverage presence and primary dimension**: the original empty slots record that no retained observation received that primary dimension. They do not necessarily show that no relevant evidence exists elsewhere in the case documents. The second pass examines the corpus under each dimension, so disagreement can expose a construct/allocation error rather than reviewer incompetence; passages with multiple purposes should not be forced into mutually exclusive substantive meaning.
- **Claim type and evidence strength**: a tender contains both invitations and requirements, an official packet can contain a supplier letter, and institutional orders can offer context without establishing named-system operation. Source authority, clause function, implementation stage and outcome validity are distinct constructs that the existing category system sometimes combines.
- **Version match and temporal coding**: the existing version field combines document-specific scope with current-version continuity, and neither of its two previously used categories establishes an operational match. Issue dates, effective dates, projected completion, historical accounts, date precision, fiscal intervals and cached pages can yield different defensible date selections.
- **Supplier identity and financial stage**: named invitees, technical supporters, training providers and reported award counterparties are not equivalent roles. Company-name variants need legal-identity evidence; estimates, bid security, sanctions, reported costs and payment arrangements must not be pooled. The new financial-stage sets are explicitly retrospective first-analyst projections of free-text fields, not silently relabelled original independent codes.
- **Governance relevance, oversight and redress**: a common URL is easy to reproduce but does not resolve legal applicability or compliance. General policy scope, manually approved outputs, a monitored payment process, a call centre and an AI-specific challenge procedure require distinct interpretation. Independent narrative notes test those distinctions without giving the reviewer the original conclusions.
- **Verification flags and source adequacy**: the earlier independent-outcome and compliance flags have no positive variation. Agreement on “Not established” may be trivial or driven by inaccessible sources; it is not proof of validity, and damaged extraction may make apparently exact text matches unreliable.

The Safe Kerala order illustrates a language/extraction problem: the earlier release acknowledges badly damaged Malayalam extraction and only a bounded original-page visual check. The new pack supplies tokens and original-page pointers without the first analyst's translation, so an appropriately qualified reviewer may dispute the reconstruction or leave it unreadable; numeric agreement cannot substitute for clause interpretation ([original Kerala order](https://document.kerala.gov.in/documents/cabinetdecisions/cabinet2104202314:52:28.pdf)).

The court training packet contains both foundation and private-limited supplier names, and OCR damage affects one scheduled session day. The review must distinguish identity uncertainty and scheduled activity from a demonstrated agreement or completed training, without being supplied an expected classification ([original training packet](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec030b6ace9e8971cf36f1782aa982a7/uploads/2026/09/2026091091.pdf)).

## What agreement will and will not establish

The comparison tool computes per-variable paired denominators, observed agreement, unweighted Cohen's kappa, coder marginals and confusion tables for nominal variables. Kappa uses the coders' marginal category frequencies, so skewed prevalence and differing marginals affect interpretation; coding reliability does not establish construct validity ([Hallgren, Computing Inter-Rater Reliability for Observational Data](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3402032/)).

Unresolved, unreadable, skipped and missing responses are excluded from complete pairs and reported separately, while an explicitly coded unknown remains a substantive category. Complete-case comparison does not establish that source unavailability is random; a high score from easy accessible records may leave the most consequential ambiguities untested ([de Raadt et al., Kappa Coefficients for Missing Data](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6506991/)).

The tool returns no kappa when there are no pairs or chance agreement is one; no universal acceptability threshold or overall “quality score” is supplied. Dates and amounts receive diagnostic exact-text agreement, financial-stage sets receive exact-set and Jaccard agreement, and reconstructed statements/party roles/remedies require qualitative adjudication.

It also suppresses kappa for coverage: the legacy allocation flag and the new corpus-presence audit are non-equivalent constructs. Their comparison can expose a flaw in the dataset, but must not be called intercoder reliability without a new first-analyst pass under the same definition.

Observations are counted once rather than once per case link, but units still share documents and some systems share a procurement. The report is descriptive, without naive row-independent significance tests or confidence intervals. No national inference, independent-outcome certification or completed second-coder result follows from this prepared pack.

## Adjudication without rewriting history

Freeze both initial passes before unblinding. Inspect disagreements and unresolved items, review the preselected agreed-item control sample, and separate definition defects, source drift, missing context, transcription, identity uncertainty and interpretation errors. Both coders' values remain in the ledger; an unsupported item stays unresolved.

A named adjudicator records a valid resolution, rationale, original-page evidence, decision date and both coders' acknowledgements. Consensus is a separate export and never replaces the initial pairs for agreement measurement; a later dataset correction needs a traceable change ledger rather than silent edits to the existing release.
