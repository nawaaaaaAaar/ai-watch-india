## Remaining limits

- Six purposively selected cases are not a complete or representative inventory of all Indian public-service AI systems.
- No institution, vendor, registry, patient, candidate or complainant was contacted; no information request or complaint was filed.
- Official self-reporting and vendor accounts are not independent operational or performance audits.
- Negative findings are restricted to the source corpus and documented search scope; current October details may remain unresolved.
- New snapshots are selected excerpts, not complete page archives or original PDF binaries. Hashes verify those UTF-8 extracts only.
- No legal/clinical advice, safety/compliance score, causation finding or validated user-demand claim.
- Notes are browser-session memory only; export before reload. No account, analytics, remote notebook upload or model call is added.
- The embedded evidence bundle remains comparatively large: the current JavaScript build is about 1.41 MB raw / 296 kB gzip. Vite emits its advisory chunk-size warning; this is not a failed build. Code-splitting may be useful before a broad public launch.
- This is the curated release, not live monitoring, arbitrary document uploads or authenticated editorial collaboration.
